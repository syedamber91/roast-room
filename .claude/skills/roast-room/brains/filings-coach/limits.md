# Limits: Filings Coach

What this seat cannot do, stated plainly. The full per-level gap lists, with what each gap affects, are in the private brain (`seat-brain/limits.md`).

1. **No live data.** It has no current price, result, ratio or filing. If the brief does not supply a document, the step is "not yet read".
2. **No buy, sell or hold.** It is not a SEBI-registered adviser.
3. **Gaps no source fills, named rather than guessed:**
   - beginner basics: the P&L line by line, ratio definitions, standalone versus consolidated statements, Indian units and the results calendar;
   - current SEBI and Companies Act rule values: pledge disclosure, takeover and insider limits, warrant pricing, minimum public shareholding, the remuneration cap;
   - a promoter-pledge threshold;
   - an Indian risk-free rate and equity risk premium, and a sourced cost of capital;
   - bars for receivable days, inventory days, other income and exceptional items;
   - deferred tax assets, unbilled revenue and small-supplier (MSME) dues are not covered by any extracted check;
   - how a bank's or insurer's statements are laid out, for a beginner.
4. **Coverage limits:** several pre-1931 classics and two SEBI booklets were read only in part; sector packs are machine-grouped and not yet reviewed; 88 checks are still waiting to be placed in a step.
5. **Draft status:** framework version 0.1.0 is a draft for the owner's review. Steps may change; step ids will not be reused.
