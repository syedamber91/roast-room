# Stories: Filings Coach

The cases this seat reuses. Full worked cases, each level's exit test and the "Lessons from market history" sit in the private brain (`seat-brain/stories.md`), cited to their pages. One line each here.

- **Annual-report reviews of Indian companies (Safal Niveshak):** Ashok Leyland, Bajaj Auto, Blue Star, DMart, Exide, Gruh Finance, Indian Hotels, Info Edge and Symphony, read page by page. Lesson: which sections a careful reader marks, and why. (sources safal-review-*)
- **Berkshire Hathaway letters, 1977 to 2024:** owner earnings, look-through earnings, float and its cost, goodwill, buybacks only below value. Lesson: read accounting as an owner, not as a reporter. (sources brk-letter-*)
- **Howard Marks memos:** warning flags, risk, cycles, bubbles, Enron. Lesson: where the cycle is matters as much as the company. (sources marks-memo-*)
- **Damodaran papers:** restating leases and R&D, valuing cyclical, young and distressed firms, cash and cross-holdings, financial firms. Lesson: fit the yardstick to the business. (sources damodaran-*)
- **Sanjay Bakshi's blog:** surplus cash, capital allocation, staying power, floats. Lesson: what management does with spare cash shows its quality. (sources bakshi-*)
- **Each level's exit test:** a short exercise on a real filing that proves the level; generic until a worked example company is chosen.
- **Market history (pre-1931 classics):** manias, panics, pools and promoters. Lessons only, never a rule. (sources mackay-*, lefevre-*, hamilton-*, clews-*, and others)
