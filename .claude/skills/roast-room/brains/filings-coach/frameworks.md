# Frameworks: Filings Coach

The leveled reading framework, version 0.1.0 (draft for owner review): 8 levels, 278 steps. This public file lists only the levels and their tasks. Each step's full detail (what to open, check, why, red and green flags, any cited number, sources, done-when) is in the private brain: `../learning-vault-invest/filings-coach/seat-brain/levels/L<n>.md`. Cite steps by id.

## Level 1: Find the documents and know what is in them
- FC-L1-T1 Get the right documents from the right place (5 steps)
- FC-L1-T2 Read in the right order (6 steps)
- FC-L1-T3 Balance sheet and shareholding basics (5 steps)
- FC-L1-T4 Cash flow and results basics (2 steps)
- FC-L1-T5 Compare like with like (2 steps)
- FC-L1-T6 Check where every claim comes from (4 steps)

## Level 2: Check the core numbers
- FC-L2-T1 Measure growth so it cannot fool you (5 steps)
- FC-L2-T2 Find where the growth came from (4 steps)
- FC-L2-T3 Read margins and operating leverage (6 steps)
- FC-L2-T4 Check that profit turns into cash (5 steps)
- FC-L2-T5 Check debt and whether the business can carry it (5 steps)
- FC-L2-T6 Measure returns on capital (6 steps)

## Level 3: Test whether the profit is real
- FC-L3-T1 Check that profit turns into cash (5 steps)
- FC-L3-T2 Separate one-off profit from repeatable profit (6 steps)
- FC-L3-T3 Read the accounting choices behind profit and assets (6 steps)
- FC-L3-T4 Test the balance sheet: debt, hidden debt and new shares (6 steps)
- FC-L3-T5 [Lenders] Test whether a bank, NBFC or insurer's profit is real (6 steps)

## Level 4: Check the people, the group and the paper trail
- FC-L4-T1 Map the group: related parties, subsidiaries, associates and restructuring (6 steps)
- FC-L4-T2 Read the auditor, the governance pages and the people who leave (6 steps)
- FC-L4-T3 Read what the promoter and other holders do with their shares (6 steps)
- FC-L4-T4 Read corporate actions and offer documents (6 steps)
- FC-L4-T5 Check the regulator record, rating reports and outside evidence (6 steps)
- FC-L4-T6 Find the concentration risks the filings name (5 steps)

## Level 5: Judge management by its own record: what it said, what it delivered and what it did with the money
- FC-L5-T1 Gather management's own words and test them against outside facts (6 steps)
- FC-L5-T2 Keep a scorecard of guidance against delivery (6 steps)
- FC-L5-T3 Test the visibility claims: orders, contracts and approvals (6 steps)
- FC-L5-T4 Read the language: tone, candour and evasion (6 steps)
- FC-L5-T5 Read the capital-allocation record (6 steps)
- FC-L5-T6 Owners, pay, board and people (6 steps)

## Level 6: Put the company in its industry and its cycle
- FC-L6-T1 Size the market and separate the industry's tide from the company's swim (6 steps)
- FC-L6-T2 Map the value chain and the competition (6 steps)
- FC-L6-T3 Place the business in its cycle (6 steps)
- FC-L6-T4 Watch supply: new capacity, new entrants, substitutes and the capital cycle (6 steps)
- FC-L6-T5 Read the cycle early: customers, gauges, money and prices (6 steps)
- FC-L6-T6 Regulation, policy and exposure outside the company's control (6 steps)

## Level 7: Read what the price already assumes, and how much room it leaves
- FC-L7-T1 Read the price tag, and pick the yardstick that fits the business (6 steps)
- FC-L7-T2 When today's earnings mislead (5 steps)
- FC-L7-T3 Do growth and return on capital justify the multiple? (5 steps)
- FC-L7-T4 Reverse valuation: read what the price expects (5 steps)
- FC-L7-T5 Split the company: sum of the parts, holdings and deal prices (5 steps)
- FC-L7-T6 Price versus value, and the margin of safety (6 steps)

## Level 8: Decide, monitor, and know when the case is broken
- FC-L8-T1 Before any money goes in: write the case, its exits and its size (6 steps)
- FC-L8-T2 Re-read the filings every quarter and on every event (6 steps)
- FC-L8-T3 Read the price chart: set-up, stage, volume and strength (6 steps)
- FC-L8-T4 Check whether the source's price-based exit rules have fired (5 steps)
- FC-L8-T5 Read the market as a whole: breadth, new issues, crowd mood, outside shocks (4 steps)
- FC-L8-T6 Watch your own behaviour: the decision log and the common biases (6 steps)
