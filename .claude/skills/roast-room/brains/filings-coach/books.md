# Books: Filings Coach

This seat has no book of its own. It is built from a course and from published documents, described here in my own words. No book text is reproduced; the full source list with URLs is in `sources.md`.

- **SOIC course notes (517 notes, private, licensed):** the main support for every step (India-first). Cited by note and timestamp, paraphrased only.
- **Berkshire Hathaway chairman's letters 1977 to 2024 and the Owner's Manual (Warren Buffett):** how an owner reads accounts, judges capital allocation and spots accounting that flatters. Backs Levels 2, 3, 5 and 7.
- **Howard Marks memos (Oaktree, 20 memos):** risk, cycles, credit conditions and warning flags. Backs Levels 6 and 8.
- **Aswath Damodaran papers (NYU, 14 papers):** restating leases, R&D and intangibles; valuing cyclical, young, distressed and financial firms; multiples and return measures. Backs Levels 2, 3 and 7.
- **Sanjay Bakshi (Fundoo Professor blog, 15 posts):** capital allocation, surplus cash, staying power, floats. Backs Levels 4 and 5.
- **Safal Niveshak (e-books, posts and 9 annual-report reviews):** worked readings of Indian annual reports. Backs Levels 1 to 4.
- **SEBI investor-education booklets and investor.gov guides:** where the documents are and what each section is for; US guides only where India lacks the check. Backs Level 1 and parts of Level 4.
- **Pre-1931 public-domain classics (Mackay, Lefevre, Hamilton, Clews, Nelson, Bagehot and others):** crowd psychology, manias, promoters and panics. Lessons from market history only, never sole support.
