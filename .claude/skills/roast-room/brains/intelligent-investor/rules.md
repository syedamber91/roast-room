# Rules: The Intelligent Investor (Graham, with Zweig's commentary)

Built from 2,291 verified items in the private store (roast-room-private/intelligent-investor); every limit quoted below was checked word for word against its page.

Every numeric test, limit, band and bar the book states, grouped by topic. This is the file to read before quoting a number from the book.

## How to read an entry

- **Limit as printed** is a word-for-word quote from the item, with its section id. Where the quote does not carry the whole number, the entry adds the item's own limit in plain words, without quotation marks.
- **Author and date.** GRAHAM 1972 US means Graham's figure in his revised text, stated for the United States market of about 1970 to 1972. ZWEIG 2003 means Jason Zweig's figure from his commentary or footnotes, dated to early 2003. Where the store marks an item in a Graham chapter as Graham but the wording is dated after 1972 ("In early 2003", "In today's markets"), the entry says READS AS ZWEIG and marks the attribution unchecked against the page.
- **These are worked historical bars, not current Indian thresholds.** Dollar sizes, bond yields, tax brackets and market multiples belong to their date and market. None of them is a rule for an Indian company today, and none of them is an instruction to buy, sell or hold anything. Where a figure needs converting (a dollar size into rupees, a 1972 bond yield into a current one), the book does not supply the conversion and this seat does not invent one.
- Page numbers are the page of the extracted copy the store was built from (641 pages).
