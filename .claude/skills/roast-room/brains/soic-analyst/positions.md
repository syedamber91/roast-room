## When to sell
- **Said, by date:**
  - 2026-07-28 (S001) and 2026-07-27 (S114): the system exit fires only when three things happen together: price below the 30-weekly EMA, relative strength over 26 or 52 weeks turns negative, and the volatility stop (ATR length 10, multiplier 2 or 2.5) turns negative. It gives up "10% to 25% from the top" (S001, FRAMEC 01:47:39, 2026-07-28).
  - 2026-07-27, fundamental exits: "Peak margins, peak profitability, peak sentiment" (S111, FRAMEC 01:02:30, 2026-07-27); "the reason you stocked it is not materializing" (S111, FRAMEC 01:10:44, 2026-07-27); repeated governance lapses, an auditor leaving or a whole board resigning; a stock that keeps doing capex with no growth over 5 to 10 years is "permanently pregnant or never delivers" (S111, FRAMEC 02:12:04, 2026-07-27).
  - 2026-07-27: a straight-line earnings record with an absurd triple-digit P/E: "Usually it's time to say Tata bye bye khatam." (S111, FRAMEC 01:22:33, 2026-07-27). Accept it is "okay to also leave money on the table" (S111, FRAMEC 00:56:09, 2026-07-27).
  - 2026-07-14: "if you know why you bought it, then the exit will be very clear" (S006, HMVC1 L671, 2026-07-14).
- **Where the figures differ:** V-stop multiplier is 2 or 2.5 in S114; 2.5 or 3 for compounders and 2 for cyclicals in S095; 3 or 3.5 for a 10 to 15 year horizon in S093 (all 2026-07-27).
- **Not stated:** an exact-peak rule. The course says the Stage 3 to 4 turn is clear only in hindsight and expects a "10 to 15% discrepancy" from the top (S001, CLASSE 01:16:41, 2026-07-28).
- **Sources:** 7 (S001, S114, S111, S006, S095, S093, S107)

## Stop losses

Satellite holdings without stops can be "destroyed very quickly" (S096, TECHNK 01:59:40, 2026-07-27). Use the weekly chart: a daily chart stock "will go for 10-15% correction more often without hitting your stop loss on the weekly chart" (S259, CAPITC 00:35:35, 2026-07-26).
  - 2026-07-14: "isn't underperformance ever the stop loss?" (S006, SB2 L158-162, 2026-07-14).
- **Not stated:** a fixed percentage loss stop. The V-stop is volatility-based.

## Promoter holding and actions
- **Said, by date:**
  - 2026-07-27: the institutional-sponsorship screen flags promoter holding that changed by more than 1 or 2 percent, then asks how the promoter bought: open market, preferential issue or warrants (paraphrase, S123, HOWC 01:28:07).

Rising stakes are stronger evidence when backed by real expansion (a course example, not a hard cutoff, S434, CLASSD 00:03:56). Warrants, EXAMPLE: the promoter pays 15 percent up front and the rest within about 18 months, "skin in the game" (S434, CLASSC 00:24:21, 2026-07-27).

## Related-party transactions and governance exits
- **Said, by date:**
  - 2026-07-27: related-party transactions above 10 percent of sales call for suspicion, and "companies routing 35% to 40% of their profit after tax (PAT) through related parties are generally considered uninvestable" (S068, MODULJ 00:11:01, 2026-07-27).
  - 2026-07-14: the same course also says "We are not accountants. We are business people." (S008, section 5, 2026-07-14) and downplays RPT paranoia where the commercial logic is sound. A genuine RPT shows healthy OCF to EBITDA, debtor days that show collection and dividends paid (S054, 2026-07-27).
  - 2026-07-27: a mass exodus of auditors or executives is an "amber flag" (S052, MODULE 00:01:10, 2026-07-27); ignoring it is one of the "cardinal sins" (S052, MODULE 00:11:19). Exit triggers: repeated governance lapses, an auditor leaving, the board resigning (S111, FRAMEC 01:09:26).
  - 2026-07-27: severe criminal cases or fraud mean instant rejection; routine tax disputes may be tolerated because nearly all top-500 companies have contingent tax liabilities (paraphrase, S456, MASTED 02:07:53). Contingent liabilities above 20 percent of net worth are a flag (S049).
- **Where it differs:** S068 sets numbers (10 percent of sales, 35 to 40 percent of PAT); S008 argues commercial logic can excuse RPTs. The notes do not reconcile them.
- **Sources:** 6 (S068, S008, S054, S052, S111, S456)
