# Principles: India Market Analyst

30 rules of thumb for a retail decision on a listed Indian stock, most-used first. Built from 55 public sources: 23 earnings calls (2024 to 2026), SEBI and NFRA documents and orders, the LODR master circular, the Kotak committee report, IiAS voting guidelines and five recent articles. Each rule is a way to question a company before buying, selling or waiting. None is advice on a named stock.

## How to read an entry

- **Sources** counts the distinct source ids that state or apply the rule. Confidence: high = 3 or more sources, medium = 2, low = 1.
- Quotation marks mean a verbatim quote with its source id. Everything else is paraphrase. Lines that are my reading of several sources say "inferred from".
- Company and market figures are perishable and carry the date of the call or document. Rules carry the date of the document and may have changed. Check the current text before relying on a threshold.
- S011 and S025 forbid quoting. They are paraphrased and carry no quotation marks.
- Allegation and finding are kept apart. An interim order is not a finding.

---

1. **Treat guidance as a company target. Write it down, note when it moves, and test it against the next result.**
   - "So normally, we don't give any guidance about what could be the future." (S014, call 2026-05-08)
   - "My team and I will commit to both the value of PAT and the margin growing in Q3." (S015, call 2025-11-10)
   - Sources: 15 (S002, S003, S006, S009, S010, S012, S013, S014, S015, S016, S018, S019, S020, S021, S022). Confidence: high.
   - Note: one bank gave 14% to 15% and later 13% to 15% in the same call (S003, 2026-04-18).

2. **Strip one-offs, reversals, recoveries, write-backs and non-operating gains out of a quarter before judging it.**
   - "It has to reverse. there is no way." (S012, call 2025-08-07)
   - "adjusted for that, the core yield was somewhere around 18%-18.5%." (S007, call 2025-11-13)
   - Sources: 14 (S003, S005, S007, S008, S009, S010, S011, S012, S013, S014, S015, S020, S022, S023). Confidence: high.
   - For S011, the same lesson holds on a pharma call where other income and a reversal sat inside the margin (paraphrase of S011).

3. **Check every figure that matters against the filed results. A call is an edited record and its numbers are sometimes garbled.**
   - "This document has been edited for readability and is not a verbatim record" (S009, 2025-10-13)
   - "The transcript has been edited wherever required for clarity, correctness of data or transcription error." (S023, 2026-01-22)
   - Sources: 13 (S001, S003, S005, S007, S008, S009, S010, S014, S015, S016, S018, S020, S023). Confidence: high.
   - Note: one speaker gave a quarter-end date a year in the future on a call held on 2025-11-10 (S015).

4. **Date every rule and re-read its current text. Regulations change and the sources disagree on old limits.**
   - "This amendment was made effective from a prospective date of April 1, 2022 and also provided a glide path till April 1, 2023." (S039, order 2025-09-18)
   - "within a period of one year from the date of occurrence of the cause of action" (S035, 2024-10) against "Lodge a complaint on SCORES within three (03) years from the date of cause of complaint." (S036, 2022-09-30)
   - Sources: 10 (S024, S034, S035, S036, S039, S043, S045, S046, S053, S055). Confidence: high.
   - Inferred from S035, S036, S043, S046: a threshold from an older document can be wrong today.

5. **Ask what a growth rate is measured against: the base year, the scope, the exclusions.**
   - "Growth in consolidated sales was around 7% excluding the seasonal portfolio, while the reported growth was 1.7%, impacted by the seasonal portfolio." (S013, call 2025-07-31)
   - "30% peak to non-peak period" (S020, call 2024-11-14)
   - Sources: 9 (S009, S012, S013, S014, S017, S019, S020, S022, S023). Confidence: high.
   - Inferred from S012, S013, S017: a duty cut, last year's destocking or a part-year acquisition each distort the base.

6. **Follow money between related parties to its final use. Approved and disclosed does not mean fair.**
   - "A transaction may be disclosed and formally approved without necessarily being fair to the company." (S049, article 2026-10-27)
   - "details of the source of funds in connection with the proposed transaction" (S053, master circular 2023-07-11)
   - Sources: 9 (S038, S039, S043, S044, S045, S046, S049, S053, S055). Confidence: high.
   - Note: S049 is a secondary article; use it for orientation and read the rule text.

7. **Separate allegation, interim order, final order and appeal. They carry different weight.**
   - "An important distinction must be maintained between allegations recorded in interim proceedings and findings finally established after adjudication." (S049, 2026-10-27)
   - "Such cases need to be kept separate by stage, because allegation, interim order, final order and appeal before the Securities Appellate Tribunal (SAT) carry different weight." (S051, October 2026)
   - Sources: 9 (S038, S039, S042, S043, S044, S049, S051, S054, S055). Confidence: high.
   - Appeal status was not checked for any order in these sources.

8. **Read profit against cash and debt: EBITDA to PAT, operating cash flow, receivable days.**
   - "if you see, Rs. 90 crores of EBITDA at quarter level but the PAT which is being there that is just around Rs. 15 crores." (S021, call 2024-08-13)
   - "Our total DSO, including unbilled, is currently at 78 days" (S009, call 2025-10-13)
   - Sources: 7 (S009, S010, S015, S017, S020, S021, S022). Confidence: high.
   - The sources state no cash-conversion bar. Operating cash flow to net income of 133% (S009) and operating cash flow to EBITDA of 85% to 90% (S017) are examples, not thresholds.

9. **Read the promoter pledge level, its stated reason and any invocation, every quarter.**
   - "specifically disclose detailed reasons for encumbrance if the combined encumbrance by the promoter along with PACs with him equals or exceeds either 50% of their shareholding in the company or 20% of the total share capital of the company" (S034, FAQ to 2022-03-30)
   - "the percentage of pledge is also increasing" (S021, call 2024-08-13)
   - Sources: 7 (S021, S028, S029, S034, S038, S040, S053). Confidence: high.
   - Note: a family holding vehicle can hold the pledge and hide it from the listed company's own column (S040, order 2018-11-02).

10. **Compare the purpose given for money raised with where the cash went.**
    - "This was in variance to the disclosure dated January 15, 2020 made by FRL to the exchanges that the proceeds will be utilized to meet the capital expenditure for the acquisition of in-store retail infrastructure assets." (S043, order 2026-05-12)
    - "either transferred back to the Company itself or routed to entities that were directly or indirectly related" (S038, confirmatory order 2025-07-30)
    - Sources: 6 (S024, S038, S043, S044, S045, S053). Confidence: high.
    - The Future Retail order found disclosure and approval defaults. It did not establish fraud. The Gensol facts are prima facie only.

11. **Do not act on tips, assured returns or unregistered advisers. Verify the registration and the payee.**
    - "It is illegal to act as Investment Adviser without SEBI registration." (S024, undated booklet)
    - "many of them are effectively enticing their followers/investors/prospective investors to purchase financial products, services, or securities in return for undisclosed compensation from producers or platforms." (S033, board memo July 2024, a proposal)
    - Sources: 6 (S024, S025, S027, S028, S033, S037). Confidence: high.
    - S025 adds the Ponzi pattern: high return with little risk, returns too smooth, no registration (paraphrase of S025).

12. **Read a lender's credit cost together with stage 3, coverage, cover and recoveries. Check the base of each ratio.**
    - "our NIM has expanded because our yields have improved, not because of benefit of cost of funds" (S002, call 2025-07-15)
    - "if you see the CD ratio excluding the refinance, then we are 80%, right?" (S008, call 2026-01-20)
    - Sources: 6 (S002, S003, S005, S006, S007, S008). Confidence: high.
    - Note: the same lender reported 89% and 80% credit to deposit ratios (S008).

13. **Follow a legal, regulatory or governance matter to the filing or order. The call often only mentions it.**
    - "The legal review, which is what we had committed at the time when we went to the press, is in process." (S001, call 2026-04-18)
    - "Love, please go through that exchange filing. This has nothing to do with Muthoot Finance, no loss incurred by the investors." (S007, call 2025-11-13)
    - Sources: 5 (S001, S003, S004, S006, S007). Confidence: high.

14. **Read an auditor or independent director leaving early as a signal, and read the filed reasons.**
    - "The resignation of an auditor before the expiry of the term may be a cause for concern." (S045, report 2017-10)
    - "Auditor resignation has been a cause of concern for investors, especially when the resignations take place just before the completion of accounts." (S046, guidelines 2023-24)
    - Sources: 5 (S004, S045, S046, S047, S053). Confidence: high.
    - S053 (2023-07-11) requires reasons within 24 hours of receipt.

15. **Most individual derivatives traders lose. Size, costs and experience do not fix that.**
    - "Around 91% of individual traders incurred net losses during FY25-FY26" (S030, study 2026-08)
    - "Longer trading experience was not associated with improved outcomes" (S031, study 2026-08)
    - Sources: 5 (S024, S030, S031, S032, S048). Confidence: high.
    - "Please note that derivatives are high risk products and mainly used for hedging purposes. This is not recommended for retail investors." (S024)
    - The studies cover derivatives, not buy-and-hold in the cash market.

16. **Know your complaint route and its clock.**
    - "Entities shall resolve the Complaint and upload the ATR on SCORES within 21 calendar days of receipt of the Complaint." (S035, 2024-10)
    - "Immediately question your TM about any transaction that you do not understand or you did not authorize your trading member." (S036, 2022-09-30)
    - Sources: 5 (S025, S027, S035, S036, S037). Confidence: high.

17. **Ask which counterparty, permission or concession the revenue depends on.**
    - "Similarly, I mean, our largest port as well, concession is due at that time." (S017, call 2026-04-30)
    - "Thus, majority of the revenue from operations of these companies (except FMNL) was linked to the operations of FRL." (S043, order 2026-05-12)
    - Sources: 5 (S017, S019, S020, S038, S043). Confidence: high.
    - Inferred from S017, S019, S020, S038, S043.

18. **Treat company-built market sizes and share claims as soft.**
    - "studded market, our best estimate, and this could vary based on whom you ask the question to, is around INR 75,000 crores, give or take here and there" (S012, call 2025-08-07)
    - "Talking from memory, but that is what it is." (S016, call 2026-02-09)
    - Sources: 5 (S012, S013, S016, S018, S019). Confidence: high.

19. **Test any story that needs a tailwind to continue: a high gold price, an incentive, recoveries.**
    - "Rs. 10 crores, very miniscule because the gold price is there." (S007, call 2025-11-13)
    - "PLI in the 30.7% gross margin was only two points." (S018, call 2025-11-06)
    - Sources: 5 (S003, S007, S012, S018, S019). Confidence: high.
    - Inferred from S003, S007, S012, S018, S019: ask what the number is when the tailwind reverses.

20. **Ask for what the pack leaves out: the balance sheet, the segment table, the old-format comparatives.**
    - "But we don't have the balance sheet." (S019, call 2024-07-19)
    - "we are not yet reporting it as a significant independent segment." (S022, call 2025-11-04)
    - Sources: 5 (S013, S015, S016, S019, S022). Confidence: high.

21. **Look at what insiders did and when: sales before bad news, trading plans, closed windows.**
    - "no new shares have been bought during the UPSI period" (S054, interim order 2025-05-28, allegation only)
    - "a cooling off period of 120 calendar days is mandated from the date of public disclosure of the trading plan till the commencement of trading by the insider" (S029, FAQ 2024-12-31)
    - Sources: 4 (S029, S040, S041, S054). Confidence: high.

22. **A rating or a regulatory filing is not a safety certificate. Compare with what the market is paying.**
    - "This would indicate that markets were already pricing in a much lower credit quality for DHFL." (S042, order 2023-04-20)
    - "SEBI does not take any responsibility either for the truthfulness or correctness of any statement, financial soundness of acquirer, or of PACs, or of the Target Company" (S034, FAQ to 2022-03-30)
    - Sources: 4 (S028, S034, S038, S042). Confidence: high.

23. **Use your vote and your shareholder rights.**
    - "Right to contribute in key corporate governance decisions through postal ballot/ e-voting." (S027, undated)
    - "only 24 resolutions out of 4840 were rejected" (S050, 2025-12-11, an opinion blog)
    - Sources: 4 (S027, S045, S046, S050). Confidence: high.

24. **An order or a headline is not revenue. Ask for the base orders, the price and the delivery schedule.**
    - "Orders were pretty strong. I think 25%, base orders 9%, but large orders is something which we got." (S014, call 2026-05-08)
    - "no reference to the price of the vehicle or delivery schedules" (S038, order 2025-07-30)
    - Sources: 4 (S014, S018, S021, S038). Confidence: high.

25. **Do not buy because the crowd is buying.**
    - "No impulsive buying or panic selling." (S027, undated)
    - "Don’t invest on the basis of market sentiments, rather analyze about the company and its financials and invest smartly." (S024, undated)
    - Sources: 4 (S024, S027, S028, S051). Confidence: high.
    - S051 (October 2026) reports individuals sold 50% of allotted IPO shares by value within a week of listing.

26. **A first disclosed loss number is an estimate. Wait for the outside review and the restated results.**
    - "because this is an internal number that we've arrived at, which is an approximate number, but not an exact number." (S004, call 2025-03-10)
    - "I do not represent that this 23.25% is perfectly accurate and precise" (S041, order 2023-11-30)
    - Sources: 3 (S004, S041, S054). Confidence: high.

27. **A legal win on definitions is not a clean bill for the structure.**
    - "related party transactions by themselves are not prohibited in law and are a common form of business transactions" (S039, order 2025-09-18)
    - "to 'look at' a transaction rather than 'look through' when it is a legitimate one" (S055, order 2025-09-18)
    - Sources: 3 (S039, S043, S055). Confidence: high.
    - Both orders judged past loans by the rules of the time. The 2021 amendment, effective 2022-04-01, would reach similar routes now.

28. **In an IPO, find the fresh issue and offer-for-sale split and compare the issue price with each seller's cost.**
    - "Every rupee went to the sellers through an offer for sale (OFS) , in which existing holders sell their shares rather than the company issuing new ones." (S051, October 2026)
    - "In this kind of an issue, the funds of the investors will go to such sellers of the shares and not to the company." (S024, undated)
    - Sources: 3 (S024, S026, S051). Confidence: high.

29. **Oversubscription and grey market premium are sentiment, not value.**
    - "oversubscription measures how many people wanted in at a given price, not whether that price was sound or whether the demand was genuine" (S051, October 2026)
    - "GMP comes from unofficial, unregulated grey-market dealing; exchanges do not publish it." (S052, 2026-10-28)
    - Sources: 2 (S051, S052). Confidence: medium.
    - S052's author sells the data used, so its numbers are unaudited.

30. **Watch promoter and executive pay, royalty and warrants against profit.**
    - "In a warrants issue, 25% of the conversion price is paid up front, with an option to convert the warrants into equity shares anytime during the next 18 months." (S046, 2023-24)
    - "Where royalty payout levels are high and exceed 5% of consolidated revenues, the Committee believes the terms of conditions of such royalty must require shareholder approval." (S045, 2017-10)
    - Sources: 2 (S045, S046). Confidence: medium.
    - The 5% royalty test appears as in force in S046 (2023-24). The 2017 report cannot say what was adopted as written.
