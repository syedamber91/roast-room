# Books: India Market Analyst

Built from the 55 public sources listed in sources.md. This seat has no books. It is a method seat built from public documents, so this file lists the key documents by kind and what each teaches. ## 1. Earnings-call transcripts (S001 to S023)
- Teach how analysts question management and how management answers. They cover lenders (S001 to S008), IT, pharma and consumer (S009 to S014), industry and new-economy firms (S015 to S021) and the Adani Enterprises and Zee calls (S022, S023), all dated 2024 to 2026. Every figure on them is perishable. S011 (Dr. Reddy's, 2025-10-24) forbids quoting without the company's written consent, so the seat paraphrases it.

## 2. SEBI investor booklets and decks
- **S024, Booklet on Securities Market (undated).** How the market, an IPO, a preferential issue, the demat account and a complaint work, plus a short list of cautions on tips, advisers and derivatives. Some procedural rules in it have since changed.
- **S025, Financial Education Booklet (November 2020).** Personal-finance basics and the Ponzi pattern: guaranteed high returns, steady returns, no registration. Paraphrase only (booklet forbids reproduction).
- **S026, Beginner's Guide to the Capital Markets (undated).** What risk and return mean, where equity returns come from, and the ranking of claims in a wind-up.
- **S027, Introduction to Securities Markets deck (undated).** Four basic questions before buying a stock (revenue, profit, debt, peers) and the shareholder rights a holder can use.
- **S028, Financial Education Part A (July 2010).** Do not buy what you cannot explain; review the reason for holding. Its figures are stale and conflict with S025.

## 3. SEBI and NFRA FAQs and regulator reports
- **S029, Insider trading FAQs (dated 2024-12-31).** Pledge counts as trading; trading plans need a 120-day cooling-off; six-month contra-trade bar; Rs 10 lakh disclosure threshold.
- **S034, Takeover regulations FAQs (to March 2022).** Open offer triggers and pricing, and the pledge disclosure rules: reasons at 50% of the promoter holding or 20% of capital.
- **S035, SCORES FAQs (October 2024)** and **S036, Investor grievance deck (2022-09-30).** How a complaint is made and how the timelines changed from three years and 30 days to one year and 21 days.
- **S037, SEBI Annual Report 2025-26, chapter 8 (August 2026).** The complaint counts, the verify-before-you-pay campaign and investor protection fund balances. Perishable.
- **S033, SEBI board memo on finfluencers (July 2024).** A proposal, not an enacted rule: regulated persons should not associate with unregistered advice-givers.
- **S047, NFRA order on the DHFL branch auditor (2023-04-13).** What a weak branch audit file looks like: appointments not approved at the AGM, no loan testing, papers added late.

## 4. SEBI F&O studies
- **S030, Profitability of individual traders in equity derivatives (August 2026).** About 91% of individual traders lost money in FY25 to FY26; costs and size against holdings explain much of it. Descriptive, not causal.
- **S031, Trading behaviour of individual traders (August 2026).** Experience did not improve results; repeated losers repeated; options selling hides tail risk.
- **S032, Comparative study of growth in derivatives against cash (July 2025).** The measures of October 2024 to May 2025 and the FY25 loss figures.
- **S048 (2026-08-20)** is a magazine's report of the same studies; check it against S030 and S031.

## 5. The LODR master circular
- **S053, Master circular for compliance with LODR (2023-07-11).** The disclosure calendar a retail holder can check: results in 45 and 60 days, default disclosure within 24 hours of the 30th day, auditor resignation reasons within 24 hours, half-yearly related-party reporting, and the quarterly deviation statement on issue proceeds. Later circulars replaced it in part.

## 6. The Kotak committee report
- **S045, Report of the Committee on Corporate Governance (October 2017).** Recommendations on independent directors, auditors, related parties, royalty above 5% of turnover, promoter pay and ratio changes of 25% or more. These were proposals with start dates of 2018 to 2020; the file cannot say which were adopted as written.

## 7. IiAS voting guidelines
- **S046, IiAS Voting Guidelines 2023-24.** What a proxy adviser treats as a warning sign: auditor tenure, director independence and attendance, warrants, borrowing limits, related-party approvals, royalty and pay. Its thresholds are IiAS views unless the text says it is law. The 2022 vote data in it is perishable.

## 8. The main orders
- **Governance and diversion.** S038 (Gensol, confirmatory order, 2025-07-30): loan end-use and pledges, at the prima facie stage. S043 (Future Retail, 2026-05-12): related-party approvals and use of funds. S044 (Suzlon, 2026-05-29): gains on group sales and circular entries.
- **Related-party routing and the short-seller report.** S039 and S055 (Adani-linked orders, 2025-09-18): allegations not established on the rules then in force.
- **Insider trading.** S054 (IndusInd Bank interim order, 2025-05-28): ex parte, prima facie. S040 (Satyam, 2018-11-02) and S041 (Satyam, 2023-11-30): disgorgement on remand.
- **Ratings.** S042 (CARE Ratings, 2023-04-20): rating committee process and the DHFL rating.
