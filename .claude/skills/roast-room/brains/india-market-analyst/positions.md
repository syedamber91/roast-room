# Positions: India Market Analyst

Built from the 55 public sources: 23 earnings-call transcripts (2024 to 2026), SEBI investor material, SEBI studies and orders, the LODR master circular (2023-07-11), the Kotak committee report (2017-10), IiAS voting guidelines (2023-24) and five recent articles. This seat is a method seat, not a person. A position here is what the sources say, dated, never a claim by the seat about any company. Regulations change: every rule carries the date of its document. Company figures are PERISHABLE. A topic with no evidence says no recorded position. Where two sources disagree, both are shown. Nothing here says to buy, sell or hold.

Contents: management guidance; guidance misses and moved ranges; management credibility and non-answers; promoter pledge; promoter and insider selling; insider trading rules; related-party deals; auditor resignation; independent directors; dilution through QIP, rights, preferential issues and warrants; buybacks; dividends; F&O trading; IPOs; grey market premium; tips and finfluencers; grievance routes; conglomerate and group structures; lenders' asset quality; regulator action and orders; shareholder voting; short-seller reports; credit ratings; retail participation; holding through a bad quarter; one-offs and earnings quality; disclosure cuts and new metrics; cash flow against profit; debt and covenants; order books and bookings; litigation and arbitration; executive pay and royalty; which rule is current.

## Management guidance
- **Stance.** Guidance is a company target stated as a range and restated each quarter. Some firms give none. A guide is not a rule and carries no audit.
- **Said, by date.**
  - 2025-07-15: "we have basically a policy of not providing guidance as such" (S002).
  - 2026-05-08: "So normally, we don't give any guidance about what could be the future." (S014)
  - 2025-08-07: "11 to 11.5 still remains our guidance on which we are trying to work on" (S012).
  - 2025-10-13: "we are keeping the company-level guidance unchanged at 3%-5% in constant currency terms." (S009)
  - 2024-07-23: "We have said 26 and 28% with an upward bias towards 28%." (S005)
- **Read (inferred from S002, S005, S009, S012, S014).** A guidance range is the start of a question: against what base, with which items added back, and restated when. Perishable; each range is dated on its call.
- **Sources:** 5 shown (S002, S005, S009, S012, S014); more guidance lines sit in S006, S007, S015 to S023.

## Guidance misses and moved ranges
- **Stance.** The sources show ranges that moved inside one call or between questioners, targets restated as median then minimum, and guides not reaffirmed.
- **Said, by date.**

  - 2024-11-14: an analyst asked about the 15% to 20% growth guide, "would you rather not go back to that?" and the CEO answered "I don't really know" (S020).
  - 2025-11-10: "We did not miss our guidance." (S015) An investor had said the company was confident about the labour fix the call before.
  - 2024-11-05: "We have not changed it." (S006) on a 2% group credit cost guide, while an analyst put one subsidiary near 7%.
  - 2026-04-18: the growth range went from 14% to 15% to 13% to 15% inside one call (S003).
- **Sources:** 5 (S018, S020, S015, S006, S003).

## Promoter pledge
- **Stance.** Pledge, release and invocation are all trades that must be filed. A rising pledge needs a stated reason. Rules below are as of the dated documents.
- **Said, by date.**
  - 2022-03 (SEBI takeover FAQ): a stated reason is needed once encumbrance "equals or exceeds either 50% of their shareholding in the company or 20% of the total share capital of the company" (S034). Disclosure within seven working days.
  - 2024-12-31 (insider trading FAQ): "Hence, trading would include creation/invocation/revocation of pledge." (S029)
  - 2023-07-11 (LODR master circular): the shareholding pattern asks "Whether any shares held by promoters are pledged or otherwise encumbered?" (S053).
  - 2024-08-13 (call): an investor said "the percentage of pledge is also increasing" and the founder answered with a 3 to 4 year taper and no percentage (S021).
  - 2025-07-30 (order, allegation stage): "promoters had created pledges for 75.74 Lakh shares of Gensol" (S038).
- **Read.** The word encumbrance is wide: "pledge, lien, negative lien, non-disposal undertaking" (S034). A pledge through a family holding vehicle may not show in the listed company's own column (S040, 2018-11-02).
- **Sources:** 6 (S034, S029, S053, S021, S038, S040).

## Promoter and insider selling
- **Stance.** Steady selling by insiders while the company reports growth is a flag in the orders. A sale needs a public plan, a cooling-off and an open window. No source says how much selling is too much.
- **Said, by date.**
  - 2018-11-02: the family's selling from 2001 to 2008 drew "None of the appellants have placed any material on record to suggest there were compelling reasons" (S040).
  - 2025-05-28 (interim, allegation only): "no new shares have been bought during the UPSI period" (S054).
  - 2024-12-31: "a cooling off period of 120 calendar days is mandated from the date of public disclosure of the trading plan till the commencement of trading by the insider" (S029).
  - 2024-12-31: "Thus, gift is a trade and the promoter shall be required to comply with requirement of disclosure, pre-clearance and contra trade restrictions." (S029)
- **Not stated:** a threshold for how much insider selling matters.
- **Sources:** 3 (S040, S054, S029).

## Insider trading rules
- **Stance.** No trading while holding unpublished price sensitive information. Trades by someone holding it are presumed motivated by it. A retail holder sees only the filed disclosures, not the company's internal list.
- **Said, by date.**
  - 2024-12-31: "Trading in securities while in possession of UPSI is prohibited as per the regulations." (S029)
  - 2025-05-28: "his trades would be presumed to have been motivated by the knowledge and awareness of such information in his possession" (S054).
  - 2024-12-31: "Any buy/sell trade, undertaken by a Designated Person (DP) and their immediate relatives, within 6 months of an earlier sell/buy trade, respectively" (S029) can be a contra trade.
  - 2024-12-31: "No, there is no requirement to disseminate the list of UPSI on the website of the company." (S029)
- **Rules change:** the FAQ replaces six earlier versions and was revised after a 2024-09-24 amendment (S029).
- **Sources:** 2 (S029, S054).

## Related-party deals
- **Stance.** Disclosure and approval are the test. A deal can be approved and disclosed and still not be fair. The definition widened on 2022-04-01 with a glide path to 2023-04-01.
- **Said, by date.**
  - 2023-07-11: related party loans, deposits, advances and investments are disclosed half-yearly with the end use of funds (S053); the audit committee needs "details of the source of funds in connection with the proposed transaction" (S053).
  - 2026-05-12 (finding): "In my view a guarantee given for lease rentals to a related party is a RPT under LODR Regulations as it constitutes a transfer of obligations or resources" (S043).
  - 2025-09-18 (finding): "related party transactions by themselves are not prohibited in law and are a common form of business transactions" (S039).
  - 2026-10-27 (article): "A transaction may be disclosed and formally approved without necessarily being fair to the company." (S049)
  - 2023: "the approval sought is for an indefinite amount for an undefined time period." (S046) as a flag.
- **Where sources differ.** Materiality: ten percent of consolidated turnover in the rule applied in S043 (events to FY2021); the lower of Rs 1000 crores or 10% from 1 April 2022 in S046 (2023-24). On who may vote, S049 says related parties are generally restricted from voting; S046 says they can abstain or vote against. S046 or the rule text is the safer read.
- **Sources:** 7 (S053, S043, S039, S049, S046 quoted; S055 and S045 for the definition and the promoter-group gap).

## Auditor resignation
- **Stance.** An auditor leaving early is a cause for concern. The reasons must be filed within 24 hours.
- **Said, by date.**
  - 2017-10: "The resignation of an auditor before the expiry of the term may be a cause for concern." (S045)
  - 2023: "Auditor resignation has been a cause of concern for investors, especially when the resignations take place just before the completion of accounts." (S046)
  - 2023-07-11: reasons are disclosed "not later than twenty-four hours of receipt of such reasons from the auditor" (S053).
  - 2025-03-10: an analyst asked why a problem survived "multiple layers of audit" and the CFO declined to answer then (S004).
- **Also:** tenure of the audit firm over 10 years (3 for banks) is an IiAS flag (S046, 2023).
- **Sources:** 4 (S045, S046, S053, S004).

## Independent directors
- **Stance.** An independent director who resigns early is in a unique position to see inside; the reasons should be filed.
- **Said, by date.**
  - 2017-10: "IDs are in a unique position, not being a part of the executive management but having overall insight into the functioning of the listed entity" (S045).
  - 2023: IiAS "considers directors to be non-independent once they cross a tenure of 10 years from the date of their first appointment" (S046).
  - 2023: "If the Chairperson is an executive director or part of the promoter group, half of the board of directors must be independent." (S046)
  - 2023: attendance of at least 75% is accepted for a director up for re-appointment (S046).
- **Where sources differ.** The Kotak committee proposed at least half independent for every listed entity (S045, 2017-10); S046 (2023) describes half only where the chair is executive or promoter linked.
- **Sources:** 2 (S045, S046).

## Dilution through QIP, rights, preferential issues and warrants
- **Stance.** Check who gets the shares, how much dilution, the urgency and the use of funds. Promoter warrants give 18 months of upside for 25% paid.
- **Said, by date.**
  - Undated booklet: preferential shares go to "an identified set of investors like promoters, strategic investors, employees, etc." (S024).
  - 2023: "In a warrants issue, 25% of the conversion price is paid up front, with an option to convert the warrants into equity shares anytime during the next 18 months." (S046)
  - 2025-11-10: asked about a QIP or rights issue, the MD said "I would not be able to share anything on corporate actions as of right now." (S015)
  - 2025-11-04: a partly paid rights issue of "upto INR25,000 crores" was approved that day; timing and staging went unanswered (S022).
  - 2024-08-13: "we have only allocated 60 lakh warrants" against about 1 crore planned (S021).
  - 2023-07-11: a quarterly statement of deviation in the use of money from a public, rights, preferential or QIP issue is required (S053).
  - 2026-01-22: "I think we raised only 10% of what was approved by the Board." (S023)
- **Sources:** 7 (S024, S046, S015, S022, S021, S023, S053 for the quarterly deviation statement of 2023-07-11).

## Buybacks

## Dividends
- **Stance.** Compare payout with profit and cash flow. A dividend alongside a default or weak operating cash flow is a flag.
- **Said, by date.**
  - 2023: a flag is "The company has defaulted on any of its debt obligations" (S046) while paying out.
  - 2025-10-13: a payout near 96% of net income is safe only while free cash flow stays above net income, and the call showed free cash flow at 125% of net income (inferred from S009).
  - 2024-10: a company must pay "within 30 days from the declaration of the dividend date to all its eligible shareholders" (S035).
  - 2023: the top 1000 companies must have a dividend policy "Effective 5 May 2021" (S046).
- **Sources:** 3 (S046, S009, S035).

## F&O trading
- **Stance.** The base rate is loss. The booklet calls derivatives not recommended for retail. Rules changed from October 2024.
- **Said, by date.**
  - August 2026: "Around 91% of individual traders incurred net losses during FY25-FY26" (S030).
  - August 2026: "70% of turnover occurred on the expiry day itself" (S030).
  - August 2026: "Longer trading experience was not associated with improved outcomes" (S031).
  - Undated: "Please note that derivatives are high risk products and mainly used for hedging purposes. This is not recommended for retail investors." (S024)
  - July 2025: "India continues to see relatively very high level of trading in EDS, compared to other markets, particularly in index options." (S032)
- **Caveat.** The studies are descriptive and say so; cash-market stock holding is not covered (S030, S031).
- **Sources:** 5 (S030, S031, S032, S024, S048).

## IPOs
- **Stance.** Read the offer document, the fresh issue against offer for sale split, the sellers' cost and the anchor book. Oversubscription is not value.
- **Said, by date.**
  - Undated: in an offer for sale "the funds of the investors will go to such sellers of the shares and not to the company" (S024).
  - October 2026: "oversubscription measures how many people wanted in at a given price, not whether that price was sound or whether the demand was genuine" (S051).
  - October 2026: SEBI's 2024 study of 144 IPOs found "individual investors sold 50% of their allotted shares by value within a week of listing" (S051).
  - Undated: "The limit for IPO application is Rs.2 Lakhs per transaction on UPI and is available only for retail individual investors." (S024)
- **Perishable:** offers for sale were 61.2% of about Rs 2.37 lakh crore raised from January 2025 to August 2026 (S051).
- **Sources:** 3 (S024, S051, S027 for the Rs 2,00,000 retail cap).

## Grey market premium
- **Stance.** Unofficial and not a forecast. A large premium has been a good sign of listing above issue in 2026; a thin one is close to a coin toss.
- **Said, by date.**
  - October 2026: "A GMP is an unregulated, unaudited quote from an informal market, not a forecast" (S051).
  - 2026-10-28: "A GMP above 25% never led to a listing below the issue price in 2026 (0 of 55 IPOs)." (S052)
  - 2026-10-28: "so a thin premium is closer to a coin toss than a signal" (S052).
- **Caveat.** The author of S052 sells the data, and S052 measures at opening price while S051 uses the close.
- **Sources:** 2 (S051, S052).

## Tips and finfluencers
- **Stance.** Treat a tip as a possible exit for someone holding unsellable stock. Check the registration number and whether the promoter is paid by the product.
- **Said, by date.**
  - Undated: "Do not rely on making your investment decisions on hot tips as a person who wants to offload securities which may not be marketable may be indulging in it." (S024)
  - July 2010: "Do not blindly follow market tips and rumours" (S028).
  - July 2024 (proposal only, not an enacted rule): finfluencers "are effectively enticing their followers/investors/prospective investors to purchase financial products, services, or securities in return for undisclosed compensation from producers or platforms." (S033)
  - Undated: "Don't blindly imitate investment decisions of others who may have profited from their investment decisions." (S027)
- **Also:** hot tips are an illegal activity per SEBI (paraphrase of S025, November 2020). Regulated entities now show a SEBI registration number on social media (S037, 2026).
- **Sources:** 6 (S024, S028, S033, S027, S025 paraphrase, S037).

## Grievance routes
- **Stance.** Go first to the company, broker or exchange, then SCORES. SCORES does not decide disputes and does not take company performance.
- **Said, by date.**
  - 2024-10: "within a period of one year from the date of occurrence of the cause of action" (S035).
  - 2024-10: "Entities shall resolve the Complaint and upload the ATR on SCORES within 21 calendar days of receipt of the Complaint." (S035)
  - 2024-10: mismanagement and financial performance are not SEBI matters: "Mismanagement of companies, financial performance of the company, Annual General Meeting, etc." (S035).
  - 2024-10: "SEBI will neither confirm nor deny the existence of any examinations as the same may be a price sensitive information." (S035)
  - 2022-09-30: "Lodge a complaint on SCORES within three (03) years from the date of cause of complaint." (S036) Superseded.
- **Perishable (FY2025-26):** SCORES received 61,788 complaints against 68,132 the year before (S037).
- **Sources:** 4 (S035, S036, S037, S025 paraphrase: three years, November 2020).

## Lenders' asset quality
- **Stance.** Read stage 2, stage 3, coverage and credit cost together, and separate recoveries and write-backs. The usual company tests do not apply to a lender as they do to a manufacturer.
- **Said, by date.**
  - 2025-07-15: an analyst on credit cost: "it has been just inching up over the last five-odd quarters." (S002)
  - 2024-07-23: "Gross loan loss to average AUF was 2.12%. Net was 1.99%" (S005).
  - 2026-01-20: "the large reduction of credit cost happened on account of lower slippages and higher coverage of guarantee." (S008)
  - 2025-11-13: "As you know, on NPA accounts, we do not recognize the interest." (S007)
  - 2026-04-18: "we don't have any stress in our portfolio as we speak." (S001)
- **Sources:** 5 (S002, S005, S008, S007, S001); S003 and S006 add more.

## Regulator action and orders
- **Stance.** Keep apart allegation, interim order, final order and appeal. The weight differs at each stage. A not-established finding is not a clean bill.
- **Said, by date.**
  - 2026-10-27: "An important distinction must be maintained between allegations recorded in interim proceedings and findings finally established after adjudication." (S049)
  - October 2026: "Such cases need to be kept separate by stage, because allegation, interim order, final order and appeal before the Securities Appellate Tribunal (SAT) carry different weight." (S051)
  - 2025-05-28: "The foregoing prima facie observations contained in this Order are made on the basis of the material available on record." (S054)
  - 2026-05-12: "This Order is not to be treated as findings on any issues pending before any court/ tribunal/ authority." (S043)
- **Read (inferred from S043, S044, S054).** Two May 2026 orders differ on group deals: S043 declined to find fraud on the evidence; S044 found misleading statements and overturned a lenient order. Both turn on tracing a group deal to cash.
- **Sources:** 5 (S049, S051, S054, S043, S044).

## Shareholder voting
- **Stance.** Retail holders can vote by e-voting, postal ballot and proxy and can requisition a meeting, yet retail voting is low.
- **Said, by date.**
  - Undated: "Right to contribute in key corporate governance decisions through postal ballot/ e-voting." (S027)
  - 2025-12-11: "The 2025 report by proxy firm IIAS indicates that the retail investors' voting percentage is as low as 20%" (S050).
  - 2025-12-11: "only 24 resolutions out of 4840 were rejected" (S050).
  - 2023: for shareholders to propose removing a director "they must collectively own 10% and call for an EGM by giving a special notice under Section 100 of Companies Act 2013." (S046)
- **Sources:** 3 (S027, S050, S046).

## Short-seller reports
- **Stance.** Treat a short-seller's arithmetic as a prompt to check against the company's own filings, not as a finding. SEBI's final orders on the January 2023 report found the allegations not established on the rules then in force.
- **Said, by date.**
  - 2025-09-18 (a short-seller claim quoted inside the order): "it would take Adicorp Enterprises around 900 years to earn enough to pay back the loans even without interest" (S055).
  - 2025-09-18 (finding): "I find that the allegations made against Noticees in the SCN are not established." (S039)
  - 2025-09-18: "to 'look at' a transaction rather than 'look through' when it is a legitimate one" (S055).
- **Sources:** 2 (S039, S055).

## Credit ratings
- **Stance.** A rating can lag the market. Compare the rating with the bond yield and the share price. The issuer pays the rating agency.
- **Said, by date.**
  - 2023-04-20: "This would indicate that markets were already pricing in a much lower credit quality for DHFL." (S042)
  - 2023-04-20: ratings "cannot be a subjective opinion and cannot be based solely on the information provided by the client" (S042).
  - July 2010: "The yield on a bond varies inversely with its credit (safety) rating" (S028).
  - 2025-07-30: two agencies cut one issuer to D in March 2025, one saying documents given to it "were apparently falsified" (S038, allegation stage).
- **Sources:** 3 (S042, S028, S038).

## One-offs and earnings quality

- **Said, by date.**

  - 2024-08-13: "we don't consider this as losses." (S021) of subsidiary losses.

  - 2025-11-13: "adjusted for that, the core yield was somewhere around 18%-18.5%." (S007)

## Disclosure cuts and new metrics

## Cash flow against profit
- **Stance.** Compare operating cash flow with profit and read receivable days, escrow and working capital.
- **Said, by date.**
  - 2025-10-13: "Operating Cash Flow to Net Income conversion is at 133%, and Free Cash Flow to Net Income is at 125%." (S009)
  - 2025-11-05: working capital "which we believe will get normalized in this quarter" (S010).
  - 2025-11-04: "it will be better to look at operating profit before working capital changes." (S022) An analyst should ask for cash flow after working capital.
  - 2017-10: look for any ratio that moved "wherever there is a change of 25% or more in a particular financial year, along with detailed explanations thereof" (S045).
- **Sources:** 4 (S009, S010, S022, S045).

## Debt and covenants
- **Stance.** Ask the debt definition, the covenants and where the money went. A leverage cap that stretches is not a cap.
- **Said, by date.**
  - 2024-11-05: "there is only one covenant breach on NCD, which is a very small amount." (S006)
  - 2026-04-30: "2.5x is the ceiling but even if I go 3" (S017).
  - 2023-07-11: default on a bank loan beyond 30 days must be disclosed "not later than 24 hours from the 30th day of such default" (S053).
  - 2023: "if the company raises debt to the full extent of the limit, its credit protection measures (Debt/EBITDA and/or Debt/Equity) will deteriorate significantly from current levels" (S046).
  - 2025-11-10: the MD gave long-term debt near Rs 120 to 130 crores; an analyst quoted near Rs 247 crores and the call did not reconcile them (S015).
- **Sources:** 5 (S006, S017, S053, S046, S015).

- **Sources:** 3 (S003, S023, S007).

## Executive pay and royalty
- **Stance.** Compare pay with profit growth, median employee pay, headcount and dividends. Royalty above 5% of consolidated turnover needs shareholder approval.
- **Said, by date.**
  - 2023: "Is the remuneration/ increase commensurate with the growth in profits/operations?" (S046)
  - 2023: "shareholders' approval will be required for royalty/brand payments to related parties exceeding 5% of consolidated turnover of the company as per the last audited financials" (S046).
  - 2017-10: "Where royalty payout levels are high and exceed 5% of consolidated revenues, the Committee believes the terms of conditions of such royalty must require shareholder approval." (S045)
  - 2023: "IiAS will generally recommend voting AGAINST charitable donations beyond 5% of average net profits." (S046)
- **Sources:** 2 (S046, S045). The IiAS figures are its own views unless the text says it is law.

## Which rule is current
- **Stance.** The documents disagree with each other because they are different dates. Always give the date of a rule and look for the current text.
- **Said, by date.**
  - SCORES complaint window: one year, 21-day reply (S035, 2024-10); three years, 30-day reply (S036, 2022-09-30); three years (paraphrase of S025, November 2020).
  - Deposit insurance: Rs 100,000 in the 2010 book (S028); Rs 5 lakh in the 2020 booklet (paraphrase of S025).
  - Settlement cycle: "Settlement cycle is on a T+2 rolling settlement basis w.e.f. April 01, 2003" (S024), undated and since changed for many securities.
- **Sources:** 4 (S035, S036, S028, S024).
