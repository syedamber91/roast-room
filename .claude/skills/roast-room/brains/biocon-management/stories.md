## Who leads and who speaks, 2022 to 2026
- **What happened:** the people leading the group and speaking on the calls changed several times between 2022 and 2026.
- **2022-11-15:** "I would like to start by welcoming Peter Bains as an additional director on the Board of Biocon Limited." (S001, Kiran Mazumdar-Shaw, held 2022-11-15)
- **2023-02-15:** "Shreehas Tambe was appointed CEO and Managing Director of Biocon Biologics in December 2022 following the retirement of Dr. Arun Chandavarkar" (S002, Kiran Mazumdar-Shaw, held 2023-02-15)
- **2023-11-10:** a group CEO role. "Peter will be responsible for supporting me in evolving strategy and driving synergies between the three group companies." (S004, Kiran Mazumdar-Shaw, held 2023-11-10) From then to 2025-01-31 Peter Bains opens most answers and hands detail to the business heads (S004, S005, S006, S007, S008).
- **2025-05-09:** "The appointment of Peter Bains as MD and CEO of Syngene International" (S009, Kiran Mazumdar Shaw, held 2025-05-09) and "I'm delighted to return to hosting Biocon Limited's quarterly earnings calls." (S009, Kiran Mazumdar Shaw, held 2025-05-09)
- **2026-05-08 and 2026-08-06:** the S013 participants list names Shreehas Tambe as Chief Executive Officer & Managing Director, Biocon Limited, and the S014 participants list shows Siddharth Mittal as chief executive of Syngene International where S011 and S012 list him as chief executive of Biocon Limited (paraphrase).

## Syngene: from a funding dip to a transition year (2024 to 2026)
- **What happened:** the research arm bought a biologics plant, hit a demand dip, returned to growth in January 2025, then reported reduced demand from one large biologics client.
- **2024-02-09:** "Syngene concluded the acquisition of the multimodal biologics manufacturing facility from Stelis Biopharma." (S005, Peter Bains, held 2024-02-09)
- **2024-08-09:** "Performance in Discovery Services was impacted by the dip seen in U.S. Biotech funding that has impacted the sector over the last 2 years." (S007, Peter Bains, held 2024-08-09)
- **2025-01-31:** "Syngene has returned to growth this quarter" (S008, Peter Bains, held 2025-01-31)
- **2025-08-08:** "Our Research Services business segment has been renamed as CRDMO" (S010, Kiran Mazumdar Shaw, held 2025-08-08) The Q1 FY27 deck labels the segment Services (S025).
- **2026-08-06:** "FY'27, we have clearly indicated, is a transition year for Syngene as it navigates the impact of reduced demand from the large biologics client." (S014, Kiran Mazumdar Shaw, held 2026-08-06)
- **Sources:** 6 (S005, S007, S008, S010, S014, S025)

## The launch wave of 2025 and management's own tally
- **What happened:** management said it brought the ustekinumab launch forward, then counted its launches against a five-product statement.
- **Ustekinumab, 2025-01-31:** "we have been able to accelerate the Ustekinumab launch by almost 4 months" (S008, Shreehas Tambe, held 2025-01-31)
- **The statement, 2025-05-09:** "So these are the 5 products we expect to launch in that 12-to-18-month window." (S009, Shreehas Tambe, held 2025-05-09) **Count, 2025-08-08:** "Four of those five are already now getting to launch, four of them we've launched." (S010, Shreehas Tambe, held 2025-08-08)
- **Claim, 2025-11-12:** "In Q4 FY '25, we had guided to launch five biosimilar products over the next 12 to 18 months, and I'm pleased to report that we have delivered on this commitment" (S011, Kiran Mazumdar Shaw, held 2025-11-12) The deck of that day lists four launched and denosumab as an expected imminent launch: "expect an imminent launch of bDenosumab" (S022, deck).
- **Read:** the call counts denosumab's US approval and licence as delivery, while the deck and the 2026-05-08 call place its US launch after the quarter, so the word delivered covers approval for that product (inferred from S011, S013, S022).
- **Sources:** 6 (S008, S009, S010, S011, S013, S022)

## The US Hulio (adalimumab) launch and the slow market (2023-07-01 onward)
- **What happened:** management gave a July 2023 launch date and later said the market took longer to form than planned.

- **After, 2023-11-10:** "We believe that this is an opportunity which is intact, but just shifted at this point in time." (S004, Shreehas Tambe, held 2023-11-10) Later calls blame originator formulary position (S009) and call the work unfinished (S010, 2025-08-08).
- **2026-02-13:** full and exclusive global rights taken in house from FKB (S012). Dated openings by year are in the positions file under adalimumab.
- **Sources:** 5 (S003, S004, S009, S010, S012)

## The FDA inspection saga: aspart and bevacizumab (2022 to 2025)
- **What happened:** two products waited on inspections of two sites for about two years.
- **2023-11-10:** "the FDA conducted the cGMP inspection of the Malaysia facility in July 2023, leading to observations primarily related to enhancing operational procedures and strengthening training programs." (S004, Peter Bains, held 2023-11-10)
- **2024-02-09:** "We have received a CRL from the US FDA for our biosimilar Bevacizumab or Abevmy filing as the agency was not able to visit the site for a pre-approval inspection." (S005, Peter Bains, held 2024-02-09) A supplementary letter followed, per S006.
- **2024-08-09:** "The 10 observations noted in the Form-483 were procedural in nature." (S007, Peter Bains, held 2024-08-09)
- **2025-01-31:** "Successful closure of both the Bengaluru and Malaysia facilities U.S. FDA inspections with the receipt of VAI status for both sites" (S008, Peter Bains, held 2025-01-31) and "Those CRLs were a break on those 2 major facilities for Biologics." (S008, Peter Bains, held 2025-01-31)
- **Sources:** 5 (S004, S005, S006, S007, S008)

## The Viatris biosimilars purchase (announced 2022-11-15, completed 2022-11-29)
- **What happened:** Biocon Biologics bought Viatris' global biosimilars business, paid for with new debt, convertibles and equity. Terms and funding are in the positions file under the Viatris funding topic.
- **How management framed it, 2022-11-15:** "this is a unique opportunity for the company to basically become a global leader in biosimilars" (S001, Kiran Mazumdar-Shaw, held 2022-11-15) and on speed, "we want to close the transaction quickly" (S001, Siddharth Mittal, held 2022-11-15)
- **Completion, as stated on 2023-02-15:** "Biocon Biologics successfully completed the acquisition of Viatris’ global biosimilars business on November 29, 2022." (S002, Kiran Mazumdar-Shaw, held 2023-02-15)
- **Later wording:** the full and final remittance of the deal considerations was reported on 2025-01-31 (S008, S019).
- **Sources:** 4 (S001, S002, S008, S019)

## The Serum arrangement is reworked (2023-05-24)
- **What happened:** the vaccine revenue assumed at the time of the deal did not arrive and the equity terms changed.
- **Before:** 2022-11-15, roughly USD 300 million a year of Serum vaccine revenue (S001, M.B. Chinappa); 2023-02-15, "I think right now we don't have clear visibility of the regulatory completion." (S002, Kiran Mazumdar-Shaw, held 2023-02-15)
- **After, 2023-05-24:** "we were expecting certain court approvals for completing this deal, the original deal, which didn't come through." (S003, Kiran Mazumdar-Shaw, held 2023-05-24) and "thereby giving Serum a US$300 million equity into BBL at an average valuation of US$6 billion." (S003, Kiran Mazumdar-Shaw, held 2023-05-24)
- **Sources:** 3 (S001, S002, S003)

## The Viatris transition finishes a year early (December 2023)

- **Done, 2024-02-09:** "we have now successfully completed the full transition of the acquired business in about 120 countries, fully 1 year ahead of schedule" (S005, Peter Bains, held 2024-02-09)
- **Sources:** 3 (S003, S004, S005)

## Retiring the structured investors and the credit upgrade (2025-06 to 2026-01)

## Net debt steps after the deal (December 2022 to May 2023)
- **What happened:** four sources of money brought net debt down in about six months.
- **2023-02-15:** "Biocon Limited has entered into definitive agreements with Kotak Strategic Situations Fund, for a structured funding of up to ₹1,200 Crores." (S002, Kiran Mazumdar-Shaw, held 2023-02-15)
- **2023-05-24:** "US$270 million was brought in through the stake sale in Syngene, US$130 million investment by Kotak, US$150 million conversion of loan in BPL to equity in BBL by Serum, and US$98 million investment by Edelweiss." (S003, Kiran Mazumdar-Shaw, held 2023-05-24)
- **Why it matters later:** Kotak and Edelweiss are the structured investors retired in 2025 and 2026 (see the story on retiring them).
- **Sources:** 2 (S002, S003)

## Refinancing and the bridge loan (2024-10 to 2025-01)
- **What happened:** the acquisition debt was refinanced with a bond and a loan, then a short bridge loan funded an investor's exit option.
- **2024-10-30 deck:** "Q2 highlight: Biocon Biologics successfully refinanced long term debt through a maiden USD bond issue and a new syndicated loan facility" (S018, deck)
- **2025-01-31:** "The purpose was to support meeting an obligation to an investor who exercised the liquidity option." (S008, Peter Bains, held 2025-01-31) and on the level, "There will be occasions as this is one where there will be a slight increase." (S008, Peter Bains, held 2025-01-31)
- **Sources:** 2 (S008, S018)

## The May 2025 turn: from IPO to strategic options and an equity raise
- **What happened:** on one call the Board's view on the biologics IPO changed, an INR 4,500 crore raise was announced and the year was called one of consolidation and transition.
- **Year framing, 2025-05-09:** "FY'25 has been a year of consolidation and transition, which has set us up to an exciting inflection point" (S009, Kiran Mazumdar Shaw, held 2025-05-09)
- **The IPO view:** on 2025-01-31 an IPO was one of the options (S008); on 2025-05-09 the Board looked at "other strategic options" including a merger (S009). Quotes are in the positions file under the IPO topic.
- **The raise:** "we intend to raise INR 4,500 crores through a combination of QIP and private placement" (S009, Siddharth Mittal, held 2025-05-09)
- **Sources:** 2 (S008, S009)

## The Q3 FY26 slowdown: pushback, then ownership (2026-02-13 and 2026-05-08)
- **What happened:** biosimilars growth slowed in the December 2025 quarter while plants and quality systems were upgraded; management's wording about it changed within one quarter.
- **2026-02-13, framing:** "While this moderated the pace of growth in Q3, it positions us very well for a more efficient and sustained ramp-up ahead." (S012, Kiran Mazumdar Shaw, held 2026-02-13) and to an analyst, "characterizing it as a slowing down of growth is probably something we'll have to sit down and look at." (S012, Shreehas Tambe, held 2026-02-13) and "While this pressure will take some time to fully ease, it is transient." (S012, Kiran Mazumdar Shaw, held 2026-02-13)
- **2026-05-08, ownership (newer):** "we had indicated like you rightly said that we are slowing down some of these things to get ready for a greater supply capability in the coming fiscal." (S013, Shreehas Tambe, held 2026-05-08)
- **Sources:** 2 (S012, S013)

## The merger and the single company (2026-02-13 to 2026-05-08)
- **What happened:** Biocon Biologics was to merge into Biocon, and the integration was reported done within 100 days.
- **2026-02-13:** "The strategic transformation we set in motion with Biocon Biologics’ acquisition of Viatris biosimilars business is now reaching its conclusion through the planned merger of Biocon Biologics with Biocon." (S012, Kiran Mazumdar Shaw, held 2026-02-13)
- **2026-05-08:** "the integration of our biosimilars and generics business was concluded seamlessly in under 100 days with no disruption to business operations, customers or patients." (S013, Kiran Mazumdar Shaw, held 2026-05-08) and "we now have full economic ownership of our largest growth engine." (S013, Kiran Mazumdar Shaw, held 2026-05-08)
- **Sources:** 2 (S012, S013)

## The insulin programme is paused and an inventory provision taken (2024-02-09)
- **What happened:** management paused an insulin programme, citing the US policy advisory on co-pays.
- **The reason given:** "right now, the policy advisory is that it's $35 no matter which insulin is prescribed as a co-pay" (S005, Shreehas Tambe, held 2024-02-09) and "So currently, we have paused this program because we feel pretty excited about the opportunity of Aspart and other analogs." (S005, Kedar Upadhye, held 2024-02-09)
- **Same call, a separate item:** "these are not expired products, but our assessment of the pending shelf life and ability to liquidate, again, has required us under the standards to create a provision." (S005, Kedar Upadhye, held 2024-02-09)
- **Sources:** 1 (S005)
