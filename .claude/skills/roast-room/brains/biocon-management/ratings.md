### 2022-03-09: CRISIL on Biocon Limited (BIOCON_09032022181508_Intimation_CreditRating_Biocon_09032022)

**Strengths:**
- Leading Indian biopharma company with a 40-year record; strong brands in biosimilars and chronic therapy areas, and a leading global insulin producer. (p002.txt)
- Revenue is diversified across generics, biosimilars and research services; Syngene gave about one-third of consolidated revenue and operating profit in first nine months of fiscal 2022. (p003.txt)
- Healthy biosimilar pipeline in diabetes, oncology and autoimmune areas, with approvals and launches with partner Viatris in regulated and semi-regulated markets. (p003.txt)
- Strong financial risk profile: healthy gearing, interest cover and cash and liquid investments built up through subsidiary fundraising. (p003.txt)
**Weaknesses:**
- Uncertain payoff from R&D-heavy biosimilar and novel biologic model, particularly in regulated markets; R&D spend expected to rise. (p003.txt)
- Exposure to regulatory scrutiny (US FDA, EMA, others) and intense competition in regulated markets, plus Indian drug price control effects on branded formulations. (p003.txt)
- Debt protection metrics expected to weaken near term because the subsidiary BBL will take large debt to fund the Viatris biosimilars acquisition. (p002.txt)
**Upgrade triggers:**
- Significantly high revenue growth from higher market share with profitability improving above 28%-30% on a sustained basis, giving healthy annual cash accruals. (p004.txt)
- Faster-than-expected improvement in debt protection metrics after BBL's Viatris acquisition, supported by healthier accruals and equity raising at BBL. (p004.txt)
**Downgrade triggers:**
- Revenue growth declines and operating margin drops below 20% on a sustained basis. (p004.txt)
- Delayed correction in debt protection metrics from further debt-funded capex or acquisitions or weaker cash generation, e.g. debt/EBITDA staying above 2.5 times in fiscal 2024. (p004.txt)
- Any adverse US FDA regulatory action that materially affects operating performance. (p004.txt)
**Liquidity:**
- Rated Strong: cash accrual over Rs 1,500 crore vs term debt obligation around Rs 800 crore for fiscal 2022, with unencumbered cash and securities of Rs 3,197 crore at December 31, 2021; may moderate if cash funds BBL's acquisition. (p004.txt)
**Numbers printed:**
- Viatris biosimilars acquisition total consideration by BBL: USD 3.335 billion (cash up to USD 2.335 billion + CCPS USD 1 billion) (p002.txt)
- Upfront cash payment funding: USD 2 billion: ~USD 800 million equity in BBL, remainder debt (p002.txt)
- Adjusted gearing at Dec 31, 2021: 0.6 time (p003.txt)
- Interest coverage, first nine months FY22: 26.7 times (p003.txt)

### 2024-11-26: CRISIL on Biocon Limited (BIOCON_26112024162227_SIGNEDIntimationCRISIL)

**Strengths:**
- Established biopharma position with diversified revenue: FY2024 mix generics 19%, biosimilars 58%, contract research 23%. (p003.txt)
- Healthy pipeline of biosimilars and novel biologics in diabetes, oncology, autoimmune; approvals in regulated markets. (p004.txt)
- Operational track record over four decades; operating margin 23-26% over past fiscals; healthy facility utilisation. (p004.txt)
- Syngene adds revenue diversity with sustained growth and profitability. (p003.txt)
**Weaknesses:**
- Uncertain payoff on R&D-driven biosimilar model, especially regulated markets; net R&D 10% of revenue ex-Syngene in FY2024, 14% in FY2023. (p004.txt)
- Exposed to regulatory scrutiny (US FDA, EMA) and intense competition including authorised generics and cost-competitive Indian players. (p004.txt)
- Average financial risk profile: elevated debt, RoCE subdued at low single digits as acquisition benefits yet to show. (p004.txt)
- H1 FY2025 performance modest: EBITDA margin 19.0% against 22.2% for FY2024; generics hit by pricing pressure and facility shutdown. (p002.txt)
**Upgrade triggers:**
- High double-digit revenue growth with profitability above 27-29% sustained, giving healthy cash accrual. (p005.txt)
- Faster-than-expected improvement in debt protection metrics via accrual and debt reduction at BBL through equity raise. (p005.txt)
**Downgrade triggers:**
- Lower-than-expected revenue growth and operating margin falling below 20-22% on a sustained basis. (p005.txt)
- Material delay in correcting debt metrics, for instance net debt to Ebitda staying above 3.5-3.6 times in the near term, from debt-funded capex/acquisitions, weak working capital, or refinancing to honour put options. (p005.txt)

**Liquidity:**
