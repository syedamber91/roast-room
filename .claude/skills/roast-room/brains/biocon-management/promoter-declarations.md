## What this adds and what it does not

- Four of five years carry a "no encumbrance during the year" declaration, and the quarterly filings carry the pledge flag "false" for all 25 periods from 2021-09-30 to 2026-09-30 (S032), so the two sources agree where they overlap.
- The wording covers "any encumbrance ... directly or indirectly held", which is wider than a pledge. It is the promoters' own statement, not an independent check; it is not tested against a depository record here.
- FY2022 (the year ended 2022-03-31) has no readable declaration. The quarterly filings still cover that year (S032).
- A declaration is dated at the start of the next financial year, so it says nothing about events after its date.
