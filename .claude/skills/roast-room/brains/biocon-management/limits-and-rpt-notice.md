## Approved limits and the Mylan transaction (EGM notice)

- **Item 2, special resolution: investment, loan and guarantee limit.** The notice asks shareholders to raise the Section 186 limit to Rs 20,000 crore over and above the statutory limit. The existing limit, approved at the AGM held on 2025-08-08, is Rs 8,000 crore over and above the statutory limit. The notice gives the statutory limit from the audited balance sheet at 2025-03-31: 60% of paid-up capital, free reserves and securities premium is Rs 6,778 crore and 100% of free reserves and securities premium is Rs 10,697 crore, so the maximum available under Section 186(2) without a special resolution is Rs 18,697 crore. It gives the aggregate of investments and loans made and guarantees and securities issued at 2025-09-30 as approximately Rs 14,600 crore, including an agreement to acquire compulsorily convertible debentures in Biocon Biologics Limited (BBL) issued to Edelweiss by 2026-01-30. The reason given is the proposed investment in BBL and potential future requirements. Result: 15.67% of votes against (48.24% of the public institutions' votes), meeting dated 2025-12-31 (S034, resolution 2).
- **Item 6, ordinary resolution: material related party transaction with Mylan Inc.** The company acquires 14,36,69,382 shares of BBL held by Mylan Inc. for USD 400 million in cash. Mylan Inc. is a related party of BBL, an unlisted material subsidiary of the company. The notice gives the value as about 23.55% of the company's annual consolidated turnover for the preceding financial year and 6% of Mylan's consolidated turnover (Mylan's turnover for the year ended 2024-12-31: USD 6,624,024,551). The transaction is to be completed no later than 2026-03-31, about three months from approval. Omnibus approval is not sought. The Audit Committee (independent directors only) and the Board reviewed and approved it on 2025-12-06, on the basis of a valuation report that the notice says is on the company's website. Result: 0.81% of votes against; the promoter group was interested and polled 0 votes (S034, resolution 6).
