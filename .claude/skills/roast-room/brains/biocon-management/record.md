# record: what BIOCON management said it would do, and what happened

Nothing in this file is live. It is a dated snapshot of the record. 39 dated statements from 2025-01-31 to 2026-05-08, each set against what was filed or announced afterwards.

Totals: Kept 14, Missed 5, Still open 9, Depends on reading 11. Two of the 14 kept are direction only: the stated size was missed or not tested (generics growth said to be big and said to be strong double-digit, actual +5.0% in FY26 on first-filed numbers). 'Depends on reading' means the outcome rests on a reading of the wording, not on a filed number; treat those as less settled than 'Kept' or 'Missed'.

## Kept (14)

- **Liraglutide, UK** (said 2025-01-31, due Jan to Mar 2025): Launch the weight and diabetes drug liraglutide in the UK.
  - Said: "And as Peter mentioned in his opening remarks, Liraglutide would be launched in the U.K. in the fourth quarter." (S008, Siddharth Mittal, Q3 FY25 call held 2025-01-31)
  - What happened: Biocon announced the UK launch on 28 Feb 2025. Evidence: NSE release. Strength: Firm.
- **Generics sales** (said 2025-01-31, due Jan to Mar 2025): Generics sales to drive growth in the quarter.
  - Said: "So, sum total of these would drive growth in the fourth quarter." (S008, Siddharth Mittal, Q3 FY25 call held 2025-01-31)
  - What happened: Generics revenue for the quarter was up 45.8% on the same quarter a year earlier (first-filed numbers). Evidence: Filed numbers. Strength: Firm.
- **Generics, next year** (said 2025-01-31, due FY26 (Apr 2025 to Mar 2026)): Generics, helped by liraglutide, to be a big growth driver next year.
  - Said: "But I think coming back to your point on teen's growth, that next year is going to be a big growth driver, Liraglutide contributing to that growth in U.K." (S008, Siddharth Mittal, Q3 FY25 call held 2025-01-31)
  - What happened: Generics revenue rose 5.0% in FY26 on first-filed numbers. Direction was right; the word 'big' was not tested. The company's own deck says +17% like-to-like (excluding about Rs 300 crore of one-time lenalidomide sales in the year before); scored on first-filed numbers. Evidence: Filed numbers. Strength: Firm.
