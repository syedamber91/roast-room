## Derived metrics added, and the EBITDA gap explained (2026-11-10)

New file derived-metrics.md (S049): margins, growth, ROE and ROCE, DuPont, leverage and cover, free cash flow, working capital days and price multiples for FY21 to FY26, each computed by code with its formula and inputs in `derived-metrics-FY21-FY26.csv`. Every figure is arithmetic on stored inputs and is labelled derived; a cell is blank where an input is not on file.

**The unexplained gap between rebuilt and printed EBITDA is closed.** It was a definition, not a data problem. The company's EBITDA is profit before tax, share of loss of joint ventures and associates and exceptional items, plus finance costs plus depreciation and amortisation, and it **includes other income**. Rebuilt that way it equals the stated figure in all six years (19,073; 21,829; 28,876; 41,642; 43,745; 37,978 Rs million). The earlier rebuild subtracted other income and so read 9.6 billion low in FY24 (41,642 against 32,029) and 11.1 billion low in FY25 (43,745 against 32,628). Two consequences: the company's stated EBITDA margins (26%, 25%, 27%, 22%) are on total income, not revenue from operations; and other income is now a large part of the margin story: other income was 56.8% of profit before tax in FY24, 64.0% in FY25 (it includes the Eris gain) and 76.5% in FY26, while EBITDA without other income fell from 22.4% of revenue in FY24 to 20.4% in FY26 (derived).

ar-tables.md also gained: each printed part of a split line on its own row (FY24 payables 958 + 61,762 = 62,720; FY24 capex and intangibles; FY24 revenue by region; FY26 option plans), share of loss of joint ventures and associates FY21 to FY26 and intangibles purchased FY25 and FY26, each checked on its page. An earlier version showed only the first part under the total's name.
