**EBITDA matches the company's own.** The company's definition (investor presentation, June 2026, note 3) is profit before tax plus finance costs, depreciation and amortisation and share of loss of joint ventures and associates, and it includes other income. Rebuilt that way (`ebitda_company_basis`) it reproduces the stated EBITDA in all six years: 19,073, 21,829, 28,876, 41,642, 43,745 and 37,978 Rs million. The earlier rebuild (profit before tax + depreciation + finance costs - other income) sat well below the printed figure for FY24 and FY25 because it took other income out; `ebitda_without_other_income` is that basis, shown beside it. FY25 other income of 12,082 includes the Eris gain, so the without-other-income margin is the cleaner operating read.

**Definitions to read before comparing.** `roe_average_equity` uses opening and closing equity attributable to owners (FY21 blank: no FY20 balance). `roce_*` uses year-end capital employed (total assets less current liabilities); the company's own ROCE definition is not stored. Net debt takes cash, current investments and other bank balances (non-current investments are not on file). Free cash flow uses PP&E purchases, plus intangibles purchased where on file (FY24 to FY26; FY21 to FY23 are PP&E only). Dividends paid include dividends to non-controlling interests and are not on file for FY21 and FY22. Payable days are blank for FY23 and FY25 (no total payables on file). Price multiples use the last weekly close on or before 31 March and the 31 March share count; the FY26 close is before the January 2026 issues' full effect on the average share count, and EPS is the basic EPS printed for the year. Prices are price-only (no dividends). Margins are on revenue from operations except `ebitda_margin_on_total_income_company_stated_basis`, which is the basis the company's own stated margins (26%, 25%, 27%, 22%) tie to.
