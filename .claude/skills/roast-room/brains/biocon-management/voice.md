# Voice: BIOCON management, on the record

Built from 14 earnings calls (S001 to S014, held 2022-11-15 to 2026-08-06, management turns only) and 11 investor decks (S015 to S025). This file is for spotting hedging and spin in what management says. It is not a script to imitate.

## How management talks

**Sentence shape.** Prepared remarks are long, list-like and number-heavy: a figure, a growth rate, a margin, then a reassurance. Answers in the question-and-answer part are looser and hand off by name ("Shreehas, do you want to take that?", S004, Peter Bains, held 2023-11-10). Detail is routinely sent offline (S009, S011, S013), which means the number never reaches the record.

**Debt formula.** On debt reduction the same three parts repeat from 2024-05-16 to 2024-08-09 and again in 2025-01-31: it is a priority, many options are open, no size and no date (inferred from S006, S007, S008). Intent is firm, timing is soft. Treat "will look to" as a direction, not a commitment. Later the wording gets concrete only after the money is raised (S010, S011, S013).

**Delay is described as a shift in time.** A missed catalyst is described as intact but shifted (S004, S005). The horizon is then a stated period: "this moves by a couple of quarters into 2024" (S004, Shreehas Tambe, held 2023-11-10), and later in the same call the steady state is "a couple of quarters away" (S004, Peter Bains, held 2023-11-10). When you see "shifted" or "line of sight", ask what date was said before and whether it has moved.

**Number guidance is replaced by direction.** Generics growth went from mid-teens (S002, held 2023-02-15; S003, held 2023-05-24) to low teens to high single digits (S004, held 2023-11-10) to double digit unlikely for FY24 (S005, held 2024-02-09). From 2025 the finance head says no forward guidance is given (S009), and no margin range is guided: "we don't specifically guide for any range" (S010, Kedar Upadhye, held 2025-08-08), while a mid-20s biosimilars margin is called the standing direction (S012, S014). Mark the newer wording each time.

**The measure moves to suit the quarter.** Analysts are steered to core EBITDA, not gross margin or single lines (S002, S003, S004). Weak reported margins come with a "normalized" figure from management (S010: 24% normalized; S014: 24% to 25% against 27%). A normalized number is management's, not a filed one.

**Bad news is framed as short and shared.** One-off (S002), transitory and sector-wide (S006), transient (S012), "in line with our expectations" for subdued results (S007, Peter Bains, held 2024-08-09; the deck says "inline with expectations", S017, deck). Admissions do exist and are plain when asked directly (S010: debt drags investor perception; S003: R&D ran at 16% against a guide of 12%).

**Regulators are handed back to the agency.** Timing comments are hedged by deferring to the agency's review and by hope words (S001, S006, S007, S009).

**Who answers which question.** Executive Chairperson: prepared remarks, tone, and sharp rejection of a premise (S010). Group CEO: fronts the call from 2023-11-10 to 2025-01-31, passes detail down. Biologics CEO: pipeline, share, price. Finance heads: debt, capex, cost lines (S012, S013). Commercial head: payer and share questions.

## Voice anchors

1. "We will be looking to reduce debt this year, but I can't give details of when and how much" (S006, Peter Bains, held 2024-05-16). Firm intent, no size, no date.
2. "We intend to take action this year, but I cannot be specific on timelines." (S007, Peter Bains, held 2024-08-09). Same formula three months later.
3. "We believe that this is an opportunity which is intact, but just shifted at this point in time." (S004, Shreehas Tambe, held 2023-11-10). Delay as a shift.
4. "The opportunity perhaps hasn't gone, it's just been shifted in time a little bit." (S005, Peter Bains, held 2024-02-09). Same frame, a second speaker.
5. "I keep reminding people that this business has to be looked at the core EBITDA level." (S003, Kiran Mazumdar-Shaw, held 2023-05-24). Steering to a favoured measure.
6. "underlying group financial performance, while relatively subdued, has come in line with our expectations and our previous guidance" (S007, Peter Bains, held 2024-08-09). Weak result, matched to plan.
7. "While this pressure will take some time to fully ease, it is transient." (S012, Kiran Mazumdar Shaw, held 2026-02-13). Duration and permanence in one line.
8. "as you are aware, we don't give forward-looking guidance" (S009, Kedar Upadhye, held 2025-05-09). Set against earlier numeric ranges.
9. "We have always directed this to say we will be in the mid-20s, and it will ramp up." (S014, Shreehas Tambe, held 2026-08-06). "Always" claims a continuity; check S004 and S005 for the mid-30s wording.
10. "I'd like you to take this off-line because your understanding of increased interest is not correct." (S010, Kiran Mazumdar Shaw, held 2025-08-08). Premise rejected, detail off the record.
11. "if it led you to believe anything different then I would like to correct myself." (S002, Shreehas Tambe, held 2023-02-15). A rare self-correction.
12. "You are right that the debt burden is what is dragging down the perspective or the perception of what investors feel about this acquisition." (S010, Kiran Mazumdar Shaw, held 2025-08-08). A plain admission, the benchmark for what plain sounds like.
