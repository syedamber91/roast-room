## L7-44: Operating working-capital build and capex (with intangibles) against operating cash flow

**Formula in words.** OWC = inventories + trade receivables - trade payables, year end. Build = OWC this year - OWC last year (FY22-FY26), computed like for like: last year's payables are the ones the SAME report prints (its prior-year column), and the build on the earlier report's own year-end payables is shown beside it. Capex = purchase of property, plant and equipment, and again with the purchase of intangible assets added; the free-cash reading is operating cash flow less each measure. Operating cash flow is already AFTER the cash-flow statement's own working-capital lines, so the build is NOT subtracted from it again; the build is context, and the cash-flow lines are shown beside it. Gap % = |build - cash absorbed per the cash-flow statement| / the larger of the two; a year is flagged if the gap is over 20% or the sign differs.
