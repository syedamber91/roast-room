Read before using:
- DERIVED means computed in the script from printed figures; everything else is a printed figure with its quote in the last section.
- A dividend is declared in one year and usually paid in the next. The table shows both: 'DPS as printed' is the declared amount, 'cash paid' is the cash-flow line of that year.
- The cash-flow tables (uses of cash, cover) are CONSOLIDATED unless a column says standalone. The reinvestment table's cash dividends are standalone (the parent's own payout), set against profit to owners.
- Cash-paid lines for FY17 to FY26 include dividend tax where the report says so; FY15 and FY16 print the dividend and the tax on separate lines. Consolidated lines include dividends to non-controlling interests (FY23 onward the line says so).
- Share basis changed twice: a 2:1 bonus (FY18) and a 1:1 bonus (June 2019). A DPS printed before those is on a bigger-per-share basis; the DERIVED column puts every year on today's share count (x1/6 before the 2018 bonus, x1/2 between the two bonuses).
- 'Profit printed' is the highlights-table row named PAT or Profit (FY12 to FY22: the row label does not say attributable to owners; FY23 onward the tables say attributable to shareholders). Payout uses those figures as printed, so it mixes parent dividends with whatever the profit row measures. Unchecked.
