## Other approvals (notices)

- **2024-04 postal ballot, item 6: the company and BBL.** Routine business transactions not exceeding Rs 421 crore in FY 2024-25. Investments, lending and guarantees on behalf of BBL within the Section 186 limits the shareholders had approved. An equity support agreement (ESA): the company may put funds into BBL, by subscribing to BBL securities or by a subordinated loan, up to a maximum of 1.0 times the EBITDA of the BBL group, only if conditions in the ESA are met; the notice says the ESA is valid until identified events and not beyond 2026-01-31, and guarantees run for the life of the contracts. The notice puts the quantified part at about 4% of consolidated turnover for the year ended 2023-03-31. Result: 26.40% of votes against (S034, 2024-04-23, resolution 6).
- **2024-09 postal ballot (notice 2024-08-23, results 2024-09-23), items 1 to 9.** Guarantees and security by BBL and its subsidiaries to secure re-financings raised by Biocon Biologics Global PLC and BNCL, with the principal of the re-financings together not exceeding USD 1.3 billion; re-financing tenure not above 10 years, and the guarantees and security continue for that tenure; a guarantee commission of 0.5% per annum on USD 1.3 billion or the lower amount outstanding. Result: 1.07% of votes against on every item (S034).
- **2025-02 postal ballot (notice 2025-02-27, results 2025-04-02), item 1.** A one-time transfer of business (assets, liabilities, intellectual property and contracts, excluding investments) from BBUK to BNCL, not exceeding USD 100 million (about Rs 862 crore); agreement expected by 2025-03-31, effective 2025-04-01, completed during FY 2025-26. Result: 0.01% of votes against (S034).

## What the notices say about validity, and what they do not

- The AGM approvals run from the AGM date until the next AGM, in the notices' own words: 2024-08-09 to the 47th AGM, and 2025-08-08 to the 48th AGM. The 48th AGM was held on 2026-08-06 and its nine resolutions included no related party resolution (S034). By the notices' stated tenure the 2025 AGM approvals therefore ran out on that date. The record does not show whether the company relied on another approval route afterwards (for example, transactions below the materiality threshold, or an approval filed in a document not read here).
- The 2024-04 postal ballot approvals for items 1 to 5 were for FY 2024-25 only (one year); the 2024-09 guarantees run for the tenure of the re-financings (up to 10 years); the 2025-04 business transfer was one-time; the Mylan Inc. purchase (S035) was to complete by 2026-03-31.
- Not read: the actual transactions against these ceilings (the annual report's related party notes hold the year's amounts, S027 and S026), the percentages of consolidated turnover for the 2024 AGM items and for 2025 AGM items 11 to 15, and any approval earlier than 2024.
