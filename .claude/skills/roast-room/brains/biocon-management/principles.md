# Principles: BIOCON management, on the record

18 decision rules, most-used first. Built from 14 earnings calls (management turns only, Nov 2022 to Aug 2026) and 11 investor decks (Feb 2024 to Aug 2026). Each rule is how management says it decides, in its own words, with the date it said so. It is not a claim by this seat about what the company will do. "Sources" counts distinct sources, across all batches, that state or apply the rule. Confidence: high = 3 or more sources, medium = 2, low = 1. A line marked "inferred" is a reading of several sources, not a quote.

Where management states a rule and then does or says something that sits against it, the entry says so. Promises scored against filed numbers are in `record.md`; this file does not repeat them.

1. **The first claim on free cash is debt reduction, with direction promised and size and date withheld.** "every dollar that we generate out of free cash, the first claim is going to be to reduce debt, and we are pretty serious about it." (S013, Kedar Upadhye, held 2026-05-08) "It is a clear management priority that we will look to continue to reduce that debt level." (S007, Peter Bains, held 2024-08-09) Dated statements: 2024-02-09, operating cash flow is the preferred route, "but there will be others as well" (S005, Peter Bains, held 2024-02-09). 2025-01-31, newer: "But it is not a straight line." (S008, Peter Bains, held 2025-01-31). 2025-08-08, the Executive Chairperson on investors: "You are right that the debt burden is what is dragging down the perspective or the perception of what investors feel about this acquisition." (S010, Kiran Mazumdar Shaw, held 2025-08-08). In the same call she tells an analyst not to focus on near-term perceptions about servicing the debt (S010, paraphrase). The deck and call figures are in `frameworks.md` entry 2. Sources: 10 (S002, S005, S006, S007, S008, S010, S011, S013, S014, S015). Confidence: high.

2. **Do not give hard forward guidance; give direction and a wider window.** "as you are aware, we don't give forward-looking guidance" (S009, Kedar Upadhye, held 2025-05-09) "I think we have kind of tried to avoid giving specific guidance." (S008, Shreehas Tambe, held 2025-01-31) Newer: "we don't guide specifically Tushar, but like what Shreehas said, you should budget for incremental growth from new launches more towards second half." (S013, Kedar Upadhye, held 2026-05-08). Also "we don't want to guide specifically at this stage" (S010, Kedar Upadhye, held 2025-08-08). Inferred from S006, S008, S009, S010: the rule sits beside many stated ranges (R&D share, a Syngene revenue range, a Biologics margin of 22% to 23%, Generics at 10% to 12%). The line management draws is between ranges on named measures, which it gives, and numbers for shares, contracts, debt timing and margin direction, which it withholds. Sources: 9 (S003, S006, S007, S008, S009, S010, S012, S013, S014). Confidence: high.

3. **Treat R&D as an investment held to a share of revenue, and reset the band as assets move through development.** "under normal circumstances, R&D should be a Capex rather than an expense." (S003, Kiran Mazumdar-Shaw, held 2023-05-24) "It does modulate as you move assets from early stage and into later stage development, which is more expensive." (S006, Peter Bains, held 2024-05-16) The band for Biocon Biologics was 12% to 15% (2022-11-15), around 12% (2023), 11% to 12% (2023-11-10), 8% to 9% (2024-05-16) and 7% to 9% (2025); the full dated list with quotes is `frameworks.md` entry 14. Management named one miss itself, 16% against a guide of 12% (S003). Sources: 9 (S001, S002, S003, S004, S006, S007, S008, S009, S011). Confidence: high.

4. **Protect price over market share, and budget price erosion as normal.** "we've always looked at holding onto a value maximized opportunity, rather than just chasing market shares" (S002, Shreehas Tambe, held 2023-02-15) "Growth is only when it's profitable." (S013, Shreehas Tambe, held 2026-05-08) Also: "If we pursue just market share, there is going to be a risk of losing value very quickly." (S011, Shreehas Tambe, held 2025-11-12). Later and sharper, 2026-05-08: "The market has examples, where you have tried to gain market share very quickly, but then you crash out also very fast" (S013, Shreehas Tambe, held 2026-05-08). From 2024-08-09 management also declines to guide on share (S007). Sources: 8 (S001, S002, S004, S005, S009, S011, S013, S014). Confidence: high.

(S001, Siddharth Mittal, held 2022-11-15) "So the proceeds of the fund will primarily be used to meet these obligations." (S009, Siddharth Mittal, held 2025-05-09). Dated: 2023-02-15 structured funding of up to INR 1,200 crores (S002, paraphrase); 2025-08-08, newer, the QIP repays Goldman Sachs first and "the remaining funds will be used to repay Kotak and Edelweiss as their payments become due" (S010, Siddharth Mittal, held 2025-08-08); 2026-02-13, newer, two QIPs raising nearly 1 billion dollars (S012, see `frameworks.md` entry 8). Sources: 8 (S001, S002, S003, S008, S009, S010, S011, S012). Confidence: high.

7. **Hold the IPO until integration, approvals and a right story are in place; when the market does not allow it, widen to a strategic review.** "we would look at an IPO when we have the right story and shape." (S005, Peter Bains, held 2024-02-09) Newer: "given the market volatility that we are seeing on the IPO front, I think the Board was of the opinion that we should look at other strategic options" (S009, Kiran Mazumdar Shaw, held 2025-05-09). Earlier, 2022-11-15: not before twelve months and options including demerger (S001); 2023-02-15: timing "based on a number of triggers" (S002, Kiran Mazumdar-Shaw, held 2023-02-15); 2025-01-31: "IPO clearly is one of the options" (S008, Peter Bains, held 2025-01-31); 2025-08-08: the Viatris commitment "was the best effort" (S010, Siddharth Mittal, held 2025-08-08). Inferred from S008, S009: the 2025-01-31 wording had the IPO as one option and the 2025-05-09 wording adds a merger, with the reason given as market volatility. Sources: 7 (S001, S002, S005, S008, S009, S010, S012). Confidence: high.

9. **Judge the business on core EBITDA, not on gross margin or isolated lines.** "we really look at the core EBITDA performance, not so much around gross margins" (S002, M.B. 10. **Build capacity ahead of demand, then stop big capex once the plan is covered, and name the P&L cost of the build.** "capacity will not be a constraint on the formulation side or the device side." (S011, Siddharth Mittal, held 2025-11-12) Newer: "capital allocation at this stage will only focus on how you profitably sustainably grow the business. We're not looking at very big-ticket greenfield kind of expansion because we don't need it to support the business plan that's going forward." (S013, Shreehas Tambe, held 2026-05-08). Where it cuts the other way: "the fixed cost of those facilities are in the P&L starting quarter 1, and that's what led to the loss" (S011, Siddharth Mittal, held 2025-11-12). Earlier, 2024-08-09, peptide capacity is added in step with the volume targeted over five years (S007, paraphrase). Sources: 6 (S006, S007, S011, S012, S013, S024). Confidence: high.
