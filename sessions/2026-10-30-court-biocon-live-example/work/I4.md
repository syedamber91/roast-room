### Q2 Lawyer

TO: the convict

QUESTION: Without the 4,029 Rs million net exceptional loss, what would the 3,856 Rs million profit attributable to owners have been, as one Rs million figure for the year to 2026-03-31?

WHY I ASK: Q2.1, how much of this period is one-off, in rupees. It moves from the quarter ended 2024-06-30 to the latest full year.

IF DEFLECTED: If you confirm the exceptional items but give no after-tax profit figure, I will ask for the tax and minority effect on the 4,029 Rs million, as one Rs million figure for the year to 2026-03-31.

### A2 Convict

ANSWER: As of the last refresh, my record holds no after-tax, after-minority figure for the 4,029 Rs million net exceptional loss in the year to 2026-03-31, so I cannot give the single number asked. I hold the bridge to that year's profit. Segment total 8,508 less exceptional 4,029 gives profit before tax 4,479 (derived, year to 2026-03-31). Profit for the year is 3,688 and owners' profit is 3,856. The 4,029 is net before tax and minority. Arithmetic only, with zero tax and zero minority effect on it, owners' profit would be 7,885 (derived, year to 2026-03-31). That is not a finding, because my record does not show the effect is zero.

MOVE: cite | concede

EVIDENCE:
- Filed, year to 2026-03-31, consolidated: segment total 8,508 Rs million. It equals profit before tax and before exceptional items (S026, PDF page 413 for the segment table; PDF page 347 for the profit and loss statement). Exceptional items, net, are a loss of 4,029 on the same page (S027, PDF page 413, one reader).
- Filed, year to 2026-03-31: profit before tax 4,479 (S027, PDF page 408, one reader). Derived: 8,508 minus 4,029 is 4,479, so the bridge ties to the Rs million.
- Filed, year to 2026-03-31: profit for the year including non-controlling interest 3,688 Rs million (S027, PDF page 190, Board's report, company filing). Profit attributable to owners 3,856 (S026, PDF page 347).
- Derived, year to 2026-03-31: tax 4,479 minus 3,688 is 791, if no other line sits between them (not read). Minority share 3,688 minus 3,856 is minus 168, a share of loss. Both are whole-year figures, not split by exceptional item.
- "The effective tax rate before exceptional item is at 21% in FY26, compared to 23% in FY25." (S027, p242, management discussion and analysis (management speech)). It gives no tax figure on the exceptional items.
- The notes show components in different entities: a gain of 1,842 on the derivative remeasurement, a write-off of 1,216 on retired loans and a provision of 762 on inventories, all in the year to 2026-03-31 (S027, PDF page 394 and PDF page 395, company filing). I have not summed or reconciled them to 4,029.

STATE I CLAIM: partial

WHAT WOULD SETTLE IT: The tax note of the consolidated accounts for the year to 2026-03-31, with the tax effect on each exceptional item. Also the attribution note giving the minority share of those items, and the consolidated profit and loss lines between profit before tax and profit for the year, which my record has not read. Those three give the after-tax, after-minority Rs million figure.
