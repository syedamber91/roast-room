### Q1 Lawyer

TO: Biocon's CFO

QUESTION: Of the Rs 6,597 million profit to shareholders for the quarter ended 2024-06-30, how many Rs million came from one-off items, and on what date did you first state that figure publicly?

WHY I ASK: Q2.1, how much of this quarter is one-off, in rupees.

IF DEFLECTED: If you confirm a one-off exists but give no rupee figure, I will ask for the figure in Rs million, plus the single quarter ended date by which it reverses.

### A1 Convict

ANSWER: As of the last refresh, my record holds no total one-off figure in Rs million for the quarter ended 2024-06-30, and no date on which management first stated one. It holds one dated management figure: the deck of 2024-08-09 says that quarter includes Eris income of 1,057 crore (derived 10,570 Rs million). The filing read also marks quarter-ended 2024-06-30 other income of 11,345 Rs million as a one-off. Neither is an after-tax cut of the 6,597 profit to shareholders.

MOVE: cite | concede

EVIDENCE:
- Filed: profit to shareholders 6,597 Rs million, quarter ended 2024-06-30, consolidated. It is read from the Sep 2024 filing's comparative column (numbers.md, Filed quarters).
- Filed: other income 11,345 Rs million, quarter ended 2024-06-30. The filing read flags it as a one-off. That is a record note, not management speech (numbers.md).
- "Q1 FY25 Includes income from the strategic collaboration / business transfer agreement with Eris Lifesciences of ₹1,057 Cr" (S017, deck, 2024-08-09).
- Derived: 1,057 crore is 10,570 Rs million for the quarter ended 2024-06-30. I have not reconciled it to the 11,345 other income.
- Not reconciled: four different rupee figures are attached to the Eris dealings (S004 to S007).
- Different period: consolidated exceptional items net were a loss of 4,029 Rs million in the year to 2026-03-31. They were a gain of 965 in the year to 2025-03-31 (S027, PDF page 190, filed table, one reader).
- Different period: exceptional items were -2,934 Rs million in the quarter ended 2025-12-31. They were -172 in the quarter ended 2025-06-30, as re-presented in the Jun 2026 filing (numbers.md notes).

STATE I CLAIM: partial

WHAT WOULD SETTLE IT: The results filing for the quarter ended 2024-06-30, which is the quarter's own text, not parsed in my record. It needs the note on exceptional items and other income, plus the tax and minority effect, to get the after-tax Rs million. The Q1 FY25 earnings release or deck would give the first-statement date.
