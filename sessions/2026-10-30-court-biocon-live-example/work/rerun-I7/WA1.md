### WA1 Convict

ANSWER: As of 2026-10-30, USD 400 million is not a limit in my files. The notice of 2025-12-07 gives it as the cash price for the Mylan Inc. stake in Biocon Biologics, put to a related party vote. That vote passed at the meeting dated 2025-12-31. The share count and rupee price the judge takes from the notes are not in my files, and the notice's count differs from the judge's. The cap is a different resolution, the Section 186 limit. How much of it was used by 2026-03-31 is not in my record.

MOVE: challenge (the word "limit" for the Mylan price), not in my record (the notes figures and the use of the cap)

EVIDENCE:
- The notice filed 2025-12-07, Item 6, says the company acquires 143,669,382 shares of Biocon Biologics Limited from Mylan Inc. for USD 400 million in cash, to be completed by 2026-03-31 (S035, hand-read, no tier).
- Item 6 also says omnibus approval is not sought (S035, notice filed 2025-12-07). The result on the meeting dated 2025-12-31 was 0.81% against, and the promoter group polled 0 votes because it was interested (S034, resolution 6).
- The cap is Item 2, a separate special resolution. It raises the Section 186 limit from Rs 8,000 crore, approved at the AGM held 2025-08-08, to Rs 20,000 crore over and above the statutory limit (S035).
- The statutory maximum without a special resolution was Rs 18,697 crore on the audited balance sheet at 2025-03-31 (S035).
- Investments, loans and guarantees stood at about Rs 14,600 crore at 2025-09-30 (S035). The notice gives the reason as the proposed investment in Biocon Biologics and potential future requirements, and does not tie the Mylan price to the cap.
- Item 2 drew 15.67% against, or 48.24% of public institutions' votes, at the meeting dated 2025-12-31 (S034, resolution 2).
- S035 says whether an approval lapsed or was used is not stated in any file here.

STATE I CLAIM: partial

WHAT WOULD SETTLE IT:

- A filing at or after 2026-03-31 that gives the investments, loans and guarantees total against the Rs 20,000 crore cap.
