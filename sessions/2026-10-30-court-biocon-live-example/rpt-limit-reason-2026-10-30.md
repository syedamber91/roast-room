# Why the related party ceilings exist, BIOCON (post-hearing note, 2026-10-30)

Not part of the hearing. `hearing.md` is append-only and issue I7 was not heard, so this note sits beside it. It answers one question: why does a ceiling on deals between Biocon group companies exist at all, and what ran out on 2026-08-06?

## What the record shows (counted, not recalled)

1. **Every ceiling is at or above Rs 1,000 crore.** Counted over the 26 intra-group ceilings in `rpt-approvals-notices.md` (S036): 26 of 26 are at or above Rs 1,000 crore; the smallest is Rs 1,005 crore (2024-04 postal ballot, item 1, BBL and BBUK); the next are 1,018, 1,120 and 1,122. Not one small ceiling was put to a vote.
2. **The bar the sources state.** From 1 April 2022 a related party deal is material when it exceeds the lower of Rs 1,000 crore or 10% of annual consolidated turnover (checks.md B-rule, S046: "transfer of resources exceeding Rs. 1000 crores or 10% of the annual consolidated turnover"). Before that the test was 10% of turnover alone (S043). Read from the council-analyst brain's own copy of the sources, not from memory. The original circular was not opened in this session.
3. **Why a ceiling and not a list of deals.** The same sources say the test counts deals "individually or taken together with previous transactions during a financial year" (S043). A year of supply, services and funding between two group companies cannot be priced in advance, so the notice asks for a "not exceeding" amount for one deal or a series (S036, as the notices word it). Past the bar, shareholders vote on that amount.
4. **Why group companies count at all.** S036 says each pair is a deal between subsidiaries that the notice treats as material. The notices tie the pairs to the Biocon Biologics structure: BBL and its sister companies move product, services and money between each other (the pairs listed in S036). That is a reading of S036's own words. The notice paragraph that gives the legal reason was not read.
5. **The size of the 2025 approvals.** The 2025 AGM items carry 31%, 27%, 22% and 7% of consolidated turnover where read (S036). The Mylan Inc. purchase (EGM, 2025-12-31) was 23.55% of turnover (S035). Both are far above the 10% leg, so shareholders voted even where the Rs 1,000 crore leg alone did not decide it.
6. **Why it ran out on 2026-08-06.** The AGM approvals say "from the AGM date until the next AGM" (S036). The 2025 approvals ran to the 48th AGM, held 2026-08-06. The 48th AGM had nine resolutions and no related party resolution (S034).

## What stays open (unchecked)

- **The notice's own reason text** (the explanatory statement) was not read. Five fetches failed: Biocon's policy PDF and its Q4 2025 related party filing returned HTTP 503, the BSE attachment returned 403, and the aggregator page held no notice (it asked an automated reader to sign up for an account; that was refused). Where to check: Biocon's investor page or the 2025-07-17 AGM notice on the exchanges.
- **Whether a new approval replaced the 2025 ones after 2026-08-06.** Not in the record. Two ways it could be fine without a vote: deals below the bar, or an approval in a document not read here. Neither is shown. This is also the contradiction the judge flagged: the convict said the 48th AGM had no related party resolution, which matches S034; the case book says approvals ran out then, which matches S036. The two statements agree; the open part is what replaced them.
- **Whether deals actually crossed the ceilings.** The annual report's related party notes hold the year's amounts, not matched to ceilings here.
- **Omnibus approval and the half-yearly filing.** The sources give one-year validity for an audit committee's omnibus approval (S053) and a half-yearly disclosure duty (Regulation 23(9), S053). Whether Biocon relied on either after 2026-08-06 is not in the record.

## Plain answer

The limits exist because deals between the Biocon Biologics companies are huge: every approved ceiling is over Rs 1,000 crore, and the 2025 ones are up to 31% of Biocon's yearly turnover. Past that bar the rule asks the owners to vote on an amount, and the vote lasts until the next AGM. The 2025 votes ran out on 2026-08-06 and the record does not show what covers the group's deals since. That is a gap in the record, not a finding about the company.
