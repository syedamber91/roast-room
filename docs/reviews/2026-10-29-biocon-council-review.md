# BIOCON council run: what was asked, said, debated and missed

Run: `sessions/2026-10-29-biocon-rating/` (gitignored; round-1.md, round-2.md, round-3.md, verdict.md). Seats: BIOCON, Analyst, SOIC, Buffett, Bezos, Musk, Graham. Purpose of this file: strengthen each seat's brain before the next run. Every figure below is from the run's cards or the BIOCON brain (`brains/biocon-management/numbers.md`, `positions.md`, `sources.md`). Figures marked (derived) are the Chair's own arithmetic on those rows. Nothing here was checked against a live filing.

## 1. What the council missed (ranked by effect on the call)

### M1. The "profit fell 62%" base year contains a flagged one-off, and nobody stripped it
- Cards used FY26 profit to owners 3,856 against 10,133 as the headline: Analyst ("-61.9%"), SOIC ("fell 61.9%"), Buffett ("a 2.3% margin", "gruesome"), Musk ("-62%"), Graham, Bezos.
- The BIOCON brain already says, in its own notes on the 2024-06-30 quarter, "Other income 11345 is a one-off". That quarter alone is 6,597 of the 10,133 base (65.1%, derived from numbers.md).
- On operating profit the year looks different: FY25 31,358 and FY26 34,381, up 9.6% (derived, four filed quarters each, sums tie to the report's annual figures). Revenue rose 10.9%.
- So most of the "profit halved" story sits below operating profit: a one-off in the base year, exceptional items (Dec 2025 quarter: -2,934; Jun 2025 re-presented: -172), tax, and non-controlling interest (M2).
- This is the Analyst's own framework 2 (strip one-offs from the base), not applied to the base. S2 scored 1 ("no exceptional split") when the BIOCON brain holds the split by quarter.
- Unchecked: whether the 11,345 sits inside a segment. If it does, the Biosimilars segment profit fall of 50.9% and the kill criterion of Rs 1,469m inherit the same distortion.
- Effect on the call: the debt questions still stand, so Wait is not undone, but the "earnings are collapsing" reason in the verdict is weaker than the cards made it sound.

### M2. Profit to owners is not group profit, and nobody asked about non-controlling interest
- numbers.md: profit for the period against profit to shareholders. Dec 2025: -518 against 1,438. FY25: 14,294 against 10,133. FY26: 3,688 against 3,856 (derived sums).
- The share of profit that goes to minority holders swung from about 4,161 to about -168 between the two years (derived). No card asked who holds the minority stake in the subsidiaries and what it takes.

### M3. Quarterly segment profit was never extracted, only segment revenue
- numbers.md "Segments" holds revenue by segment for 11 quarters. Segment profit exists only in the annual report (note 40).
- Graham asked for Q1 FY27 biosimilar segment profit; Bezos set a condition on it; Graham's kill criterion rests on it; the BIOCON seat answered "not on record". The filed quarterly results carry a segment results table, so this is an extraction gap, not a data gap.

### M4. S5, S6, S7 had no data, and the BIOCON seat could not fill them
- The annual report extraction read tables only; "the report's prose was not read" (numbers.md, sources.md). Governance sits in prose: directors' report, corporate governance report, auditor's report and key audit matters, related party note, notes on contingent liabilities.
- The brain holds no quarterly shareholding pattern (promoter holding, pledge or encumbrance), no related-party note, no auditor name or tenure, no board and independent director list, no audit committee, no remuneration, no Regulation 30 history beyond ten filings.
- Result: S5 and S7 scored 0 (no data) by the only two seats that scored them; S6 scored 1 on web lines the Chair added and nobody checked.

### M5. The debt questions sat behind a note nobody had read
- Both "due within a year" figures (65,476 and 40,784) came from tables. The prose of the borrowings note (covenants, undrawn lines, security, reason for the gap), the financing cash flow split and the reconciliation of current borrowings were not extracted.
- The two seats that tried to explain the gap guessed (Musk: interest or lease flows, wrong, withdrawn). The BIOCON seat could not.

### M6. No valuation inputs anywhere
- No share count, EPS, market cap, book value, P/E band or price-to-owner-earnings. Buffett asked for "a price I can set against owner earnings"; SOIC could not run D1 (P/E) or A7 (debt to equity, no equity figure); the Analyst could not run valuation at all (by design) and nobody else could.

### M7. The BIOCON call records hold management turns only
- Sources are "management's words only". The Analyst's core method is to find the hard questions that were deflected. On BIOCON that map does not exist: who asked what, and whether it was answered.

### M8. The 12-point underperformance has no cause on file
- changes.md lists ten company filings and says none gives a reason. No block or bulk deals, institutional flows, index changes, or ratings actions are on file. SOIC could not run timing (no RSI, ADX or 30-week average in the brief).

### M9. Protocol and Chair misses
- The Chair wrote "53% fall" for Biosimilars profit; the true figure is 50.9% (53.0% was total segment profit). The BIOCON seat caught it in Round 3.
- Web-sourced promoter lines entered the brief unchecked, and three seats scored S6 at 1 on them. The ladder has no "unverified source" state.
- S3 came out as seven 1s because seats outside their competence scored it from a thin brief; S3 coverage was counted from the BIOCON seat's brain, not the brief.
- The brief had no checklist of required data, so the council discovered its holes one round at a time.

## 3. What to add to each brain before the next run

### BIOCON seat (the owner of M1 to M5)
Extraction to add to the annual report and filings read (prose included, not tables only):
1. Quarterly shareholding pattern for the last 8 quarters: promoter and promoter group holding, pledged and encumbered shares, institutional holding, any change in the month. (Source: exchange shareholding filings.)
2. Related-party note (Ind AS 24), half-yearly Regulation 23 disclosures, approved ceilings against amounts, balances with group entities and the subsidiaries' minority holders.
3. Auditor: name, tenure, key audit matters, any qualification or emphasis of matter, the CARO report, any resignation. Board: directors and independent directors, committee membership, any departure in 3 years, remuneration against profit.
4. Borrowings note prose: covenants, security, undrawn and committed lines, rating actions, the reason current borrowings (40,784) differ from the maturity table (65,476), and the financing cash flow split.
5. Quarterly segment results (profit) and the segment EBITDA, not only segment revenue; mark every basis change (the 2026-06-30 row is on a different basis).
6. Exceptional items and other income by quarter with a one-off tag, profit for the period against profit to owners, and the non-controlling interest share, with what each subsidiary's minority holds.
7. Contingent liabilities, tax and litigation notes, share count, EPS, book value, equity, EBITDA (so debt to equity and cash flow to EBITDA can be run).
8. Analyst questions and management answers from the last 6 calls, with a mark for each one answered, partly answered or declined (M7).
Also add: a "figures the brief may need" index at the top of `_hot.md`, so the seat can volunteer its S2 and S3 rows without being asked.

### Analyst seat
- A required-data checklist for the brief (see section 4) and a rule: if S5, S6 or S7 data is missing, say "brief incomplete" before scoring.
- Apply framework 2 to the base year, not only to the current year.
- A new state, `unverified`, for numbers from a web or aggregator source; score such a section 0 until the filing is read.
- A BIOCON-specific question pack from the run: debt maturity reconciliation, NCI, one-off base, QIP funding, covenants.

### SOIC seat
- A data pack list of what the course gates need: weekly price series, 30-week average, RSI, ADX, relative strength, quarterly sales growth, P/E, ROCE, debt to equity, CFO to EBITDA, promoter and institutional holding. Most gates were untested only because nothing arrived.

### Buffett, Graham, Bezos, Musk
- Buffett: owner earnings inputs (operating cash flow, capex, share count, market cap). Graham: volume against price for biosimilars. Bezos: a dated launch calendar with the approval each launch depends on. Musk: the maturity ladder with the cost of each tranche.

## 4. Data pack the Chair should have before any stock rating run
Per company, dated, each line with its source named (filing, page, date), and nothing from a web search unless checked against the filing:
price and 52-week range; last 8 quarters (revenue, operating profit, exceptional items, profit for the period, profit to owners, NCI); latest annual report tables and prose; shareholding pattern and pledge; related-party note; auditor and board; borrowings maturity, covenants, undrawn lines; operating cash flow, capex, financing flows; shares, EPS, equity, EBITDA; segment profit by quarter; last 6 calls with analyst questions; filings of the last 90 days; a reason for any large price move.

## 5. Protocol fixes (stock rating mode)
1. Add the `unverified` state and treat web-sourced facts as 0 until checked.
2. Name which seats are competent to score each section (S1: Analyst, BIOCON; S2: Analyst, BIOCON, SOIC; S3 and S4: Buffett, BIOCON, Analyst; S5 to S7: Analyst, BIOCON, SOIC); a seat outside its competence writes "-".
3. Count coverage from the brief only and list any fact a seat brought from its own brain as "added in debate".
4. A brief-completeness gate: the Chair lists missing data-pack lines at the top of the brief and says whether to fetch first or run knowing the holes.
5. Chair arithmetic check on every number put into a draft resolution.
6. Re-derive any base-year figure a verdict leans on (the "fell 62%" line) before it reaches the report.
